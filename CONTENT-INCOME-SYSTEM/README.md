# Content Income System

Purpose: build a serious, safe content creation and monetization system for `The Open Door AI Systems` within 5 to 7 days.

The objective is not to spam 100 low-quality AI posts. The objective is to create a repeatable machine that can generate 100 content assets, review the best ones, schedule consistently, capture leads, and turn attention into affiliate revenue, digital products, consulting, or automation service opportunities.

## Operating Principle

Automation should create drafts, variations, CSVs, schedules, and first-response systems. A human should approve brand positioning, offers, platform connections, and anything that publishes publicly until the system proves quality and compliance.

## Core Brand

Brand: `The Open Door AI Systems`

Positioning: practical AI systems for busy small business owners, creators, ministry leaders, coaches, and working parents.

Promise: help people organize chaos, create content, automate repetitive work, and execute with clarity without hype.

Main CTA: `Comment SYSTEM if you want the simple workflow checklist.`

## Recommended Stack

Use what is already available first.

- Marky: brand voice, topics, draft post generation, campaign ideation.
- Brand2Social: scheduling, bulk CSV upload, cross-platform distribution, comment/DM features where officially supported.
- Canva Bulk Create: generate 100 static/carousel visual posts from CSV data.
- InVideo AI or Hypernatural: generate higher-quality faceless videos from scripts and brand styles.
- CapCut or Canva: final video cleanup, captions, pacing, and brand polish.
- OpenCode local system: content database, script generation, CSV generation, quality control, reporting.
- YouTube account already available: use for Shorts and eventually longer educational videos.

## First Channels

- Facebook Page: main community/business presence.
- Instagram Business account: Reels and carousel reach.
- YouTube: Shorts first, longform later.
- TikTok: optional after the system is stable.
- LinkedIn: lower volume, higher trust, career and service credibility.

Do not create multiple personal accounts. Use one real personal identity plus business pages/accounts.

## Monetization Order

1. Lead capture: checklist, template, or mini guide.
2. Affiliate offers: tools already used in the system, only after testing.
3. Service offer: `AI workflow cleanup for small businesses`.
4. Digital product: templates, prompt packs, content calendar, checklist.
5. Platform ad revenue: treat as delayed upside, not the first money plan.
