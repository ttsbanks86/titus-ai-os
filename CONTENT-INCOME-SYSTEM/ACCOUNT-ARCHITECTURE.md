# Account Architecture

## Goal

Create a manageable content network that looks trustworthy, avoids spam behavior, and can eventually monetize through leads, affiliate links, services, and products.

## Recommended Accounts

### Existing

- YouTube account: use for Shorts immediately.

### Create Or Confirm

- Facebook Page: `The Open Door AI Systems`.
- Instagram Business account: `The Open Door AI Systems` or close available handle.
- TikTok account: same brand, optional in week 1 if easy.
- LinkedIn Page: optional, but Titus personal LinkedIn is more valuable first.

## Do Not Create

- Multiple personal Facebook accounts.
- Duplicate Instagram accounts for the same brand.
- Fake persona accounts.
- Extra YouTube channels until the first channel has a real content rhythm.

## Safe Connection Order

1. Create/confirm Facebook Page.
2. Create/confirm Instagram Business account.
3. Connect Facebook Page and Instagram to Meta Business Suite.
4. Connect Brand2Social using official OAuth only.
5. Connect Marky only if needed and only through official OAuth.
6. Connect YouTube through official Google OAuth.

## DM And Comment Automation Rules

- Only reply to user-initiated comments or messages.
- Do not cold-DM people.
- Do not send repeated promotional messages outside allowed windows.
- Use automation disclosure where appropriate, such as: `Automated assistant here. I can send you the checklist or route this to Titus.`
- Keep first automation simple: comment keyword `SYSTEM` sends checklist link or asks one qualifying question.

## Content Safety Rules

- Do not post 100 nearly identical videos.
- Do not repost other creators' videos without significant commentary and rights clearance.
- Do not use fake income claims.
- Do not imply guaranteed passive income.
- Disclose affiliate relationships when links are used.
- Keep AI content educational, original, and useful.

## Brand2Social Setup Targets

Profiles to connect first:

- Facebook Page.
- Instagram Business account.
- YouTube.

Profiles to connect second:

- TikTok.
- LinkedIn.

Initial posting volume:

- Facebook: 2 posts/day.
- Instagram: 1 reel or carousel/day.
- YouTube Shorts: 1 short/day.
- TikTok: 1 short/day only after quality test.
- LinkedIn: 3 posts/week, more thoughtful and less automated.

## Human Review Gates

Approval required before:

- Connecting any social account.
- Publishing the first post on a new account.
- Turning on DM automation.
- Adding affiliate links.
- Running paid ads.
- Buying new tool subscriptions.
