# 7-Day Execution Plan

## Definition Of Done

By the end of 7 days, the system should have:

- Active brand workspace in Marky.
- Facebook Page, Instagram Business, and YouTube connected to Brand2Social.
- 100-post content database created.
- 30 to 50 approved posts scheduled.
- 10 to 20 approved videos scheduled or queued.
- One lead magnet offer ready.
- One service offer ready.
- DM/comment workflow drafted and tested safely.
- Weekly reporting dashboard in Live Cowork.

## Day 1: Foundation

- Confirm whether TikTok should be included this week.

- Build 30 short-video script ideas.
- Build lead magnet outline.

Deliverables:

## Important Reality Check

This system can be mostly automated, but income is not automatic. The first 7 days build the machine. The first 30 days test content-market fit. The first 90 days are where service leads, affiliate clicks, and audience growth become measurable.
