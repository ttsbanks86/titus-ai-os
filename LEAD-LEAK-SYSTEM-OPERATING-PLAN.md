# Lead Leak System Operating Plan

**Date:** June 2, 2026  
**Purpose:** Working plan for building and operating a text-first, call-supported lead leak recovery business.

## 1. Business Overview

The lead leak system helps local businesses recover potential customers they are losing because their intake and follow-up process is weak.

A business may be leaking leads if:

- Calls are missed and never followed up.
- Website forms do not trigger fast responses.
- Google Business Profile traffic does not convert into conversations.
- There is no text-back system.
- Voicemail, booking, or intake is confusing.
- Ad leads are generated but not followed up quickly.

Core offer:

> We find where your business is losing leads, then help you capture, follow up, and track them automatically.

## 2. Target Customers

## 3. How We Obtain Leads

### A. Google Maps

Search by niche and city, such as:

- `plumber Dallas`
- `roofing company Atlanta`
- `med spa Miami`
- `dentist near me`

Collect:

- Business name
- Phone number
- Website
- City
- Google rating
- Contact person or owner, if available
- Notes about visible lead leaks

### B. Business Directories

Potential sources:

- Yelp
- Angi
- Thumbtack
- BBB
- Chamber of Commerce sites
- Industry directories

### C. Website Audits

Look for businesses with:

- No contact form
- Broken or slow contact form
- No booking page
- No SMS option
- No chat widget
- Weak call-to-action
- No automated follow-up

### D. Missed Call / Intake Testing

Respectfully check whether the business answers, texts back, follows up, or has a clear voicemail/intake process.

Do not misrepresent ourselves, spam, or interfere with the business. Keep testing simple and ethical.

### E. Paid Lead Lists

Paid lists can be used later, but the recommended starting point is manual Google Maps and website research because it is cheaper and cleaner.

## 4. Lead Scoring

Score each prospect based on how likely they are to benefit from the service.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| No SMS option | +2 |
| No contact form automation | +2 |
| Bad website intake | +2 |
| Missed call / no answer | +3 |
| High-value niche | +3 |
| Good reviews but weak follow-up | +2 |

Prioritize businesses with the highest scores.

## 5. Outreach Strategy

Use a **text-first, call-supported** approach.

Recommended sequence:

1. Initial SMS
2. Follow-up SMS
3. Manual call attempt for warm or high-value leads
4. Final SMS
5. Book audit/demo

Example first message:

> Hey, is this the owner of {{Business Name}}? I noticed a couple places where new customer inquiries may be slipping through your website/phone flow. Want me to send over what I found?

If they reply, move them into a short conversation and book a call.

## 6. SMS and Call System

The software will usually have both:

1. **Monthly platform fee** for the CRM/automation system.
2. **Usage-based charges** for texts, phone numbers, and call minutes.

Recommended stack:

- GoHighLevel or similar CRM for SMS, calls, automations, calendars, and pipelines
- Google Sheets or Airtable for prospect lists
- Google Maps/Yelp for lead research
- Loom for audit videos
- Stripe for payments
- Calendly or GoHighLevel calendar for booking

## 9. Fulfillment Workflow

Once a business says yes:

1. Collect payment.
2. Collect business details.
3. Connect or provision phone/SMS number.
4. Set up CRM pipeline.
5. Add missed-call text-back.
6. Add website form follow-up.
7. Add appointment booking.
8. Test all automations.
9. Send client a simple dashboard or report.
10. Review results monthly.

## 10. Example Client Automation

If customer calls and the business misses the call:

> Hi, this is {{Business Name}}. Sorry we missed your call. How can we help?

If customer submits website form:

> Thanks for contacting {{Business Name}}. We received your request and will follow up shortly. Would you like to book a time here?

The business should receive a notification and the lead should be tracked inside the CRM.

## 11. Compliance Basics

Keep outreach and client systems clean.

Rules:

- Avoid spammy mass outreach.
- Contact during reasonable business hours.
- Keep messages relevant and specific.
- Do not make misleading claims.
- Respect STOP/unsubscribe requests.
- Prefer business numbers, not personal numbers.
- Include opt-out language where appropriate.
- Keep records of replies and opt-outs.

For larger-scale SMS/calling campaigns, confirm TCPA and local compliance requirements before scaling.

## 12. Simple MVP Plan

Start manually before overbuilding.

1. Choose one niche.
2. Choose one city.
3. Build a list of 100 businesses.
4. Audit website/phone/contact flow.
5. Score prospects.
6. Send personalized SMS outreach.
7. Call warm/high-value leads only.
8. Sell audit or setup package.
9. Build fulfillment in GoHighLevel or similar.
10. Charge monthly for management.
11. Repeat with another city or niche.
