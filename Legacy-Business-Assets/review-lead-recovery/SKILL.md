# Review & Lead Recovery System

## Overview
Help local businesses (home service companies) get more Google reviews and recover lost leads.

## Target Market
- Seattle area: Seattle, Bellevue, Renton, Kent, Tacoma
- Niche: Home service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, electricians, cleaning companies)

## Service Packages

### Package 1: Review Growth System ($250-500 setup, $97-197/mo)
- Google Review automation
- Review request SMS/email sequences
- Review monitoring dashboard
- Response templates

### Package 2: Lead Follow-Up System ($350-750 setup, $147-297/mo)
- Missed call text-back
- Lead response automation
- CRM integration
- Lead tracking

### Package 3: Content System ($500-1,000 setup, $197-397/mo)
- Social media content
- Google Business Profile posts
- Email newsletters
- Blog content

## Sales Process

### Step 1: Find Leads
- Search Google Maps for home services in Seattle
- Check review count (< 50 reviews = opportunity)
- Check response time (> 24 hours = opportunity)
- Check social media presence

### Step 2: Mini-Audit
- Review count vs competitors
- Response time analysis
- Social media activity
- Website quality assessment

### Step 3: Outreach
- Email: Professional, specific, value-first
- Phone: Direct, reference specific issues
- Follow-up: 3-touch sequence

### Step 4: Close
- Discovery call
- Custom proposal
- Contract signing
- Payment via Stripe

## Tools (Free Stack)
- Google Sheets: CRM
- Gmail: Outreach
- Google Forms: Lead capture
- Calendly: Booking
- Stripe: Payments
- VistaCreate: Design
- Ollama/OpenCode: AI assistance

## Compliance
- CAN-SPAM: Include unsubscribe, physical address
- TCPA: No auto-dialer, get consent for SMS
- Google ToS: No fake reviews, no review gating
