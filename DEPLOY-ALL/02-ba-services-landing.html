Yes. 30 minutes, no slides, no pitch, no follow-up sequence. If we are not a fit, I will tell you on the call and point you somewhere better. That has happened more than once.

What happens after the call?

You get a recording and a 1-page summary. If you want help fixing what we found, we can talk about a 2-week or 6-week engagement. If not, you have the gap and the next step either way.

No. I have done gap audits for mortgage brokers, real estate teams, insurance agencies, law firms, and home services. If your business has a process that needs to be cleaner, we can talk.

Most consultants deliver a 50-page deck. I deliver a 1-page summary, a working map, and a fix that you can actually run. Less ceremony, more clarity, faster results.
