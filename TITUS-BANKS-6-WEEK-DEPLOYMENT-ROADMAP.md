## The 3 things that must be true for Week 1 to succeed

1. **You must deploy the 4 sites.** Without sites, there is no lead capture. Without lead capture, the email automation has no subscribers. Without subscribers, the conversion engine has nothing to convert.

2. **You must wire the MailerLite automation.** Without the 5-email sequence, every subscriber who fills out the Gap Audit form gets a welcome email and then silence. The sequence is the conversion mechanism.

3. **You must send the first outreach batch.** Without outreach, the first 2 weeks have zero new contacts. The list does not grow itself. The first 10 emails are the hardest. The 11th is easier.

**If you do only 3 things in Week 1, do these 3.** Everything else can wait. These 3 cannot.

---

## The 3 things that must be true for Week 6 to succeed

1. **You must have taken at least 4 calls.** The KPI is booked calls per week. By Week 6, the minimum is 1 call per week. If you have not taken 4 calls by Week 6, the lead generation is broken and needs fixing before anything else.

2. **You must have a financial snapshot.** The CFO agent must produce the first monthly financial summary. Without it, you are flying blind on costs.

3. **You must have a month 2 plan built from real data.** Not from theory. From the actual metrics of the first 6 weeks. The plan must answer: What worked? What did not? What do we double down on? What do we cut?

**If these 3 things are true at Week 6, the system is working.** If they are not true, the system needs debugging before month 2 begins.
