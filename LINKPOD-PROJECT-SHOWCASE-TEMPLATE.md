# LinkPOD Project Showcase Template

Use this format for project blocks on the Titus Banks career LinkPOD page.

## Showcase Format

### Project Name

One-line summary of what the project is.

**Problem:** What issue the project solves.

**Built:** What you created, organized, automated, documented, or managed.

**Tools / Skills:** Tools, workflows, or business skills used.

**Result:** What the project makes easier, faster, clearer, or more organized.

**Proof / Link:** Add public file, screenshot, static page, or short link when ready.

---

## Example: Lead Leak System

### Lead Leak System

A missed-lead recovery workflow for local service businesses that helps owners identify where inquiries fall through the cracks and install a simple follow-up system.

**Problem:** Many small service businesses lose revenue because calls, texts, DMs, and form leads are not tracked consistently. Follow-up is often delayed, scattered, or dependent on memory.

**Built:** A practical service package with an audit offer, intake checklist, discovery script, demo workflow, tracker layout, pricing model, and outreach plan.

**Tools / Skills:** Business analysis, workflow mapping, client intake, lead tracking, process improvement, service design, documentation, and automation planning.

**Result:** Turns scattered customer inquiries into a repeatable lead-flow process that can be audited, cleaned up, and improved without requiring a business to replace all of its existing tools.

**Proof / Link:** Pending public one-page offer or project static page.

---

## Example: Career Operations System

### Career Operations System

A personal job-search operating system that organizes resumes, alerts, recruiter communication, LinkedIn, Gmail labels, USAJOBS activity, and application follow-up.

**Problem:** Job searching creates scattered tasks across email, job boards, resumes, messages, and deadlines. Without a clear system, high-value opportunities can get buried.

**Built:** A structured workflow using HOT Gmail labels, resume folders, LinkedIn updates, job alerts, USAJOBS tracking, and daily briefing support.

**Tools / Skills:** Operations management, information organization, process design, job-search strategy, documentation, automation support, and communication workflows.

**Result:** Creates a cleaner career pipeline where important alerts and follow-ups are easier to identify, prioritize, and act on.

**Proof / Link:** Pending resume PDF, LinkedIn profile, or sanitized workflow screenshot.

---

## Example: NOLO / The Open Door Content System

### NOLO / The Open Door Content System

A repeatable content operations workflow for faith and encouragement videos across TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook.

**Problem:** Short-form video production can become messy when files, edits, captions, schedules, and posted archives are not managed consistently.

**Built:** A batch workflow for raw video intake, local audio cleanup, Descript handoff, Brand2Social posting, confirmation notes, and posted-video archiving.

**Tools / Skills:** Content operations, file management, video workflow design, social scheduling, caption/title preparation, quality control, and cross-platform publishing.

**Result:** Makes it easier to prepare and publish multiple videos without losing order, duplicating files, or mixing posted and unposted assets.

**Proof / Link:** Pending public NOLO bio page or campaign link.
