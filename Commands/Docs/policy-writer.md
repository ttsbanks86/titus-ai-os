# Policy Writer

## Purpose
Draft a company policy from guidelines, regulations, or best practices.

## Inputs
- Policy topic or title
- Guideline source (company values, legal requirements, industry standards)
- Scope (who the policy applies to)
- Effective date
- Review cycle (annual, semi-annual)
- Related policies (optional)

## Outputs
- Formal policy document
- Policy summary (1-page version)
- FAQ section
- Acknowledgment form template

## Workflow
1. Define policy scope and applicability
2. Draft policy sections:
   - Policy statement (clear, enforceable language)
   - Definitions and key terms
   - Scope and applicability
   - Policy details and requirements
   - Roles and responsibilities
   - Enforcement and consequences
   - Exceptions and accommodations
   - Related policies and references
3. Create FAQ section addressing common questions
4. Generate acknowledgment form for employee sign-off
5. Include version history and approval signatures

## Example Execution
```
/policy-writer --topic "Remote Work Policy" --scope "All employees" --effective "July 1, 2026" --review "annual"

Output:
━━━ POLICY: Remote Work Policy ━━━

**Policy Number:** HR-2026-003
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2026
**Review Date:** July 1, 2027
**Owner:** HR Director
**Applies To:** All employees

## Policy Statement
[Company] supports flexible remote work arrangements that maintain productivity, collaboration, and company culture while providing employees with work-life flexibility.

## Definitions
- **Remote Work:** Working from a location other than the primary office
- **Hybrid Schedule:** Combination of in-office and remote days
- **Core Hours:** 10am–2pm local time when all team members must be available

## Requirements
1. Remote work must be approved by direct manager
2. Minimum 2 in-office days per week (unless fully remote approved)
3. All core hours must be observed
4. Home office must meet ergonomic and security standards
5. Availability on Slack/Teams during work hours

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **Employee:** Maintain productivity, attend core hours, update manager on schedule
- **Manager:** Approve requests, ensure equitable treatment, monitor performance
- **HR:** Maintain policy, handle exceptions, track compliance

## Enforcement
Violations may result in: verbal warning → written warning → remote privilege review → formal discipline

## FAQ
Q: Can I work from another state?
A: Yes, with 30 days notice and manager approval. Tax implications may apply.

Q: What if I need full-time remote?
A: Submit a formal request. Approval based on role requirements and performance history.
```

## Validation Checks
- Confirm policy language is clear and enforceable
- Verify scope matches the stated applicability
- Ensure enforcement procedures are fair and documented
- Check that related policies are cross-referenced correctly
- Validate effective date allows adequate communication time
