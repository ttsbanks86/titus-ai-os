Introduction

How to Use This Audit

Most professionals ask: "What should I be doing?" Most faith leaders ask: "What is God asking of me right now?"

This audit asks a different question: Where is the gap between what is and what should be?

You will answer 15 questions across three columns: Operations, Stewardship, and Vision.

ColumnWhat it measures
OperationsThe business and systems side. The numbers, the process, the cash flow, the people, the gaps you can name.
StewardshipThe faith side. How you are using the time, money, energy, and influence God placed in your hands right now.
VisionThe gap between where you are and where you are called to be. This is where faith and operations meet.

Scoring

Each question is scored 0 to 4.

ScoreMeaning
0Not started, no clarity
1Aware, no plan
2Inconsistent, partial
3Consistent, with gaps
4Disciplined, multiplying

Time and Setting

12 to 18 minutes. Quiet space. No phone. A cup of coffee or tea. Pen and paper or this printed copy.

Add the five scores in each column. Then read the interpretation on page 4.

What your score means

45 to 60
Multiplying
You have clarity, discipline, and alignment. The next step is not to do more. The next step is to lead and invest in others. Consider mentoring one person this quarter.
30 to 44
Growing, with blind spots
You are moving. One of the three columns is consistently below the others, and that is the gap to close first. Read the column with the lowest score twice.
15 to 29
Founder Stage
You are doing too much of the work yourself, and the foundation is not yet built. The risk is not failure. The risk is staying busy and calling it obedience. A 90-day plan with one outside advisor will change the trajectory.
Below 15
Reset
This is a season to slow down, write, and pray before adding anything new. The gap is not tactical. The gap is direction. Consider a 2-week sabbatical before the next decision.

The Three Gaps

If your lowest column is...The gap is...
OperationsYou are working in the business, not on the business. You need a Business Analyst to map and document the core process.
StewardshipYou are building at the cost of your soul, your family, or your walk with God. The business is not the problem. The lack of boundaries is.
VisionYou are doing a lot of good work, but it is not pointed anywhere. You need a 90-day vision reset, not a new strategy.
