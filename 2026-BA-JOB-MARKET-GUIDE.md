## Application Strategy

### Resume Optimization
1. **Use keywords** from job descriptions
2. **Quantify results** (numbers, percentages)
3. **Highlight healthcare experience** as differentiator
4. **Include AI/automation skills** as unique value

### LinkedIn Optimization
1. **Headline:** Include "Business Analyst" and key skills
2. **About:** Tell your story with quantified results
3. **Experience:** Use STAR method for each role
4. **Skills:** Add all relevant skills (18+)
5. **Recommendations:** Request from colleagues/managers

### Interview Preparation
1. **STAR Method:** Situation, Task, Action, Result
2. **Technical Questions:** SQL, process mapping, requirements
3. **Behavioral Questions:** Stakeholder management, teamwork
4. **Healthcare Questions:** Regulatory, compliance, patient care
