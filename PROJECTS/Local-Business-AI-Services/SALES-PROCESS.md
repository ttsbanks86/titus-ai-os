# Sales Process — Review & Lead Recovery System

---

## Overview

This document outlines the complete sales process for Local Business AI Services, from lead research to client onboarding. Follow this process for every lead.

---

## 1. Lead Research (Day 1)

### Sources
- Google Maps (primary)
- Yelp
- Facebook
- LinkedIn
- Industry directories
- Referrals

### Research Checklist
1. **Business name** — Confirm name and spelling
2. **Phone number** — Verify it's active
3. **Email address** — Find professional email
4. **Website** — Check if it exists and is active
5. **Address** — Confirm location
6. **Reviews** — Check Google review count and rating
7. **Social media** — Check if they post consistently
8. **Lead capture** — Check if they have a booking form
9. **Response time** — Test if they answer calls/emails

### Add to CRM
- Add lead to LEAD-TRACKER.csv
- Set status to "New"
- Set next action to "Send cold email #1"
- Log research date and notes

---

## 2. Cold Outreach (Day 1-3)

### Email Sequence
1. **Cold email #1** (Day 1) — Initial outreach
2. **Follow-up email #1** (Day 3) — Reference specific issue
3. **Follow-up email #2** (Day 7) — Break-up email

### Call Sequence
1. **Cold call #1** (Day 5) — After follow-up email #1
2. **Voicemail** (Day 5) — If no answer
3. **Cold call #2** (Day 7) — After follow-up email #2

### Outreach Rules
- Send from professional email address
- Personalize every email (mention business name, specific issue)
- Keep emails under 200 words
- Don't send more than 20 emails per day from new account
- Track opens and replies
- Log all outreach in LEAD-TRACKER.csv

---

## 3. Qualification (Day 3-7)

### Qualifying Questions
1. "How many Google reviews does {{Business Name}} currently have?"
2. "How many of those reviews have you responded to?"
3. "How many customers do you get per week?"
4. "How do you currently follow up with leads?"
5. "What's your average job value?"

### Qualification Criteria
**Good Fit:**
- 10-50 reviews (opportunity to grow)
- Responds to less than 50% of reviews
- Gets 10+ customers per week
- No system for lead follow-up
- Average job value $200+

**Bad Fit:**
- 100+ reviews (already established)
- Responds to all reviews
- Gets less than 5 customers per week
- Has a marketing agency
- Average job value less than $100

---

## 4. Mini-Audit (Day 3-7)

### Audit Process
1. **Complete mini-audit** using mini-audit template
2. **Send audit** to lead via email
3. **Follow up** within 48 hours
4. **Offer** free 10-minute consultation

### Audit Checklist
- Google Business Profile review
- Website review
- Social media review
- Lead follow-up review
- Identify quick wins
- Identify opportunities

---

## 5. Sales Call (Day 7-10)

### Call Structure
1. **Opening** (10 seconds) — Confirm availability
2. **Discovery** (5 minutes) — Ask qualifying questions
3. **Presentation** (5 minutes) — Show audit findings and recommendations
4. **Proposal** (3 minutes) — Recommend package
5. **Close** (2 minutes) — Ask for the sale

### Call Rules
- Keep call under 15 minutes
- Reference specific issues from audit
- Use their name at least 2 times
- Ask for the meeting before hanging up
- Log call in LEAD-TRACKER.csv immediately after

---

## 6. Proposal (Day 10-14)

### Proposal Components
1. **Executive summary** — Current state and recommendations
2. **Scope of work** — What's included and not included
3. **Pricing** — Package options
4. **Timeline** — When deliverables will be completed
5. **Why choose us** — Benefits and ROI
6. **Next steps** — How to get started
7. **Agreement** — Signature lines

### Proposal Rules
- Send proposal within 24 hours of sales call
- Follow up within 48 hours if no response
- Log proposal in LEAD-TRACKER.csv
- Track opens and replies
- Set reminder for 48-hour follow-up

---

## 7. Closing (Day 14-21)

### Closing Techniques
1. **Assumptive close** — "When would you like to start?"
2. **Alternative close** — "Would Tuesday or Thursday work better?"
3. **Urgency close** — "I have one spot open this week."
4. **Value close** — "This will bring you 3-5 new reviews per week."

### Closing Rules
- Always ask for the sale
- Don't be pushy — be helpful
- Handle objections gracefully (see objection-responses.md)
- Log close attempt in LEAD-TRACKER.csv
- Update status: "Proposal Sent" or "Call Booked"

---

## 8. Onboarding (Day 21-28)

### Onboarding Process
1. **Send intake form** — Collect business information
2. **Schedule onboarding call** — 15 minutes via Zoom or phone
3. **Collect access** — Google Business Profile, email, customer list
4. **Complete setup** — Review link, request templates, feedback form
5. **Train client** — Walk through process
6. **Deliver documentation** — Setup summary and templates

### Onboarding Rules
- Complete onboarding within 48 hours of proposal acceptance
- Log each completed step in client folder
- Send client daily updates during onboarding
- Log completion in LEAD-TRACKER.csv
- Update status: "Client — Active"
- Schedule first monthly report
