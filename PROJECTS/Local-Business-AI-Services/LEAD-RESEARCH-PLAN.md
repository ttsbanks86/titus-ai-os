## Research Sources

### 1. Google Maps (Primary)

**Search Terms:**
- "plumber near me"
- "HVAC repair Seattle"
- "electrician Seattle WA"
- "auto repair Seattle"
- "cleaning service Seattle"

**Research Checklist:**
1. Business name
2. Phone number
3. Email address (if available)
4. Website URL
5. Address
6. Google review count
7. Google rating
8. Number of unresponded reviews
9. Hours of operation
10. Photos (quality and quantity)

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- 10-50 reviews (opportunity to grow)
- Responds to less than 50% of reviews
- Has a website (but may not have booking form)
- Gets 10+ customers per week
- Average job value $200+

## Lead Scoring

### Scoring Formula

```
Lead Score = (Review Opportunity × 3) + (Response Gap × 2) + (Customer Volume × 2) + (Job Value × 1) + (Referral Bonus × 1)
```

### Scoring Criteria

| Factor | Score 1 | Score 2 | Score 3 |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| Review Opportunity | 10-20 reviews | 21-50 reviews | 51-100 reviews |
| Response Gap | 0-25% responded | 26-50% responded | 51-75% responded |
| Customer Volume | 1-5/week | 6-10/week | 11+/week |
| Job Value | $100-199 | $200-499 | $500+ |
| Referral Bonus | No referral | Warm lead | Strong referral |

### Lead Categories

| Score | Category | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 15-20 | Hot Lead | Prioritize outreach |
| 10-14 | Warm Lead | Standard outreach |
| 5-9 | Cool Lead | Low priority |
| 0-4 | Cold Lead | Skip |

## Quality Control

### Daily Review
1. Check lead count (20/day target)
2. Review lead quality (score 10+)
3. Check for duplicates
4. Verify contact information
5. Update status codes

### Weekly Review
1. Review total leads (100/week target)
2. Analyze lead sources (which sources produce best leads)
3. Review outreach results (reply rate, call rate)
4. Adjust research process based on results
5. Update LEAD-TRACKER.csv
