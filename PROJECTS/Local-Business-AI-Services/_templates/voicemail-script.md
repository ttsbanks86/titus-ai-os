# Voicemail Script — 30 Seconds

---

**Version 1: Review Focus**

"Hi {{First Name}}, this is Titus Banks. I was looking at {{Business Name}} on Google and noticed you have {{Review Count}} reviews. I help businesses like yours get more Google reviews and follow up with missed leads. I put together a quick audit of your online presence and wanted to share it with you. Give me a call back at {{Your Phone Number}} when you get a chance. Thanks."

---

**Version 2: Problem Focus**

"Hi {{First Name}}, this is Titus Banks. I noticed {{Business Name}} has {{Number}} unresponded Google reviews. I help local businesses fix that and get more reviews from happy customers. I have a few ideas that could help {{Business Name}} get more customers. Give me a call back at {{Your Phone Number}}. Thanks."

---

**Version 3: Value Focus**

"Hi {{First Name}}, this is Titus Banks. I help local service businesses like {{Business Name}} get more Google reviews and follow up with missed leads. Most businesses I work with see 3-5 new reviews per week. I'd love to show you how this works for businesses like yours. Give me a call back at {{Your Phone Number}}. Thanks."

---

**Notes:**
- Leave voicemail only after 2-3 unanswered calls
- Keep under 30 seconds
- Include your phone number twice (beginning and end)
- Speak slowly and clearly
- Log the voicemail in LEAD-TRACKER.csv immediately after
- Follow up with an email within 1 hour of leaving voicemail
- Update status: "Voicemail Left"

**Template Variables:**
- `{{Business Name}}` — Business name from Google Maps
- `{{First Name}}` — Contact name if available, otherwise use "there"
- `{{Review Count}}` — Current Google review count
- `{{Number}}` — Number of unresponded reviews
- `{{Your Phone Number}}` — Titus's phone number
