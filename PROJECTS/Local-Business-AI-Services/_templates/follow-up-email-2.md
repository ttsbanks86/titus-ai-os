**Notes:**
- Send 7 days after cold email #1
- This is the "break-up" email — create urgency
- Reference a specific metric from the mini-audit
- Offer a no-commitment value (2-minute video)
- Keep under 120 words
- If no response, move lead to "Not Interested" status
- Consider calling on Day 7 before sending this email

**Template Variables:**
- `{{Business Name}}` — Business name from Google Maps
- `{{First Name}}` — Contact name if available, otherwise use "there"
- `{{Review Count}}` — Current Google review count
- `{{Number}}` — Number of unresponded reviews
