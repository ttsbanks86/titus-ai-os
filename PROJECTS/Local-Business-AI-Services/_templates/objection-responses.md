# Objection Responses — Common Sales Objections

---

## Price Objections

**"That's too expensive."**
"I understand. Let me ask: how much is a new customer worth to {{Business Name}}? If a plumbing job is $500-2,000, and this system brings you one extra job per month, it pays for itself 1.5-2.5x over. Does that make sense?"

**"I can do this myself."**
"You absolutely can. Most of my clients are capable of doing this themselves. The question is: do you have time? You're running a business, on job sites all day. I set this up in 2-3 days and manage it monthly so you can focus on what you do best."

**"I don't have the budget right now."**
"I get it. Let me ask: what's your marketing budget right now? Most businesses I work with spend $500-2,000/month on marketing. This is a fraction of that and delivers measurable results. Would it help to start with just the basic package at $97/month?"

---

## Timing Objections

**"We're too busy right now."**
"That's actually the perfect time. This system runs in the background while you're on job sites. You don't need to do anything — I set it up and manage it. Most businesses see results in 2-3 weeks with about 30 minutes of your time for setup."

**"Call me back next month."**
"Totally understand. Can I ask what's happening next month? Is it a busy season, or something else? I ask because the businesses that start now see results by next month. If you wait, you're leaving reviews and leads on the table."

**"We're in our slow season."**
"Slow season is actually the best time to build your review pipeline. When things pick up, you'll have a system ready to capture every lead. Plus, customers leave more reviews when they're not rushed."

---

## Competitor Objections

**"We already have a marketing agency."**
"That's great. Most of my clients also have a marketing person or agency. I'm not trying to replace anyone. I just focus on one thing: getting you more Google reviews and following up with missed leads. Would it help to get a second opinion on that specific area?"

**"We already use {{Competitor}}."**
"No problem. How's that working for you? Are you getting the results you want? I ask because many businesses I work with tried other solutions but needed something more focused on reviews and lead follow-up. Can I show you how my approach is different?"

**"Our nephew handles our marketing."**
"That's smart — family is reliable. Let me ask: is your nephew getting you the results you want? Most businesses I work with have someone who 'handles' marketing, but they're not getting consistent results. Would it help to get a professional opinion on that specific area?"

---

## Trust Objections

**"I've never heard of you."**
"Fair point. I'm a local business owner here in Seattle. I help businesses like yours get more reviews and follow up with missed leads. I'd be happy to share references from other businesses I've worked with. Would that help?"

**"How do I know this will work?"**
"Great question. I put together a free mini-audit of {{Business Name}}'s online presence. I'll send it to you — no strings attached. You can see exactly what I'd fix and decide if it's worth it. Sound fair?"

**"I need to think about it."**
"Totally understand. What specifically do you want to think about? Is it the price, the timing, or something else? Sometimes talking through it helps."

---

## Decision-Maker Objections

**"I need to talk to my partner/spouse."**
"Of course. Would it help if I put together a one-page overview you can share with them? I can also jump on a quick call with both of you if that would be helpful."

**"I need to check with my business partner."**
"Totally understand. What questions do you think they'll have? I can address those now so you have everything you need when you talk to them."

---

## Closing Objections

**"Just send me more information."**
"Happy to. What's the best email address? I'll send you a quick overview of how this works for businesses like yours. Can I follow up in a day or two to see what you think?"

**"I'll think about it and get back to you."**
"No problem. When should I follow up? I don't want to be pushy, but I also don't want you to forget about this. Would next Tuesday work?"

**"Not interested."**
"No problem, {{First Name}}. Thanks for your time. If you ever want to revisit this, just give me a call. Have a great day."

---

**Notes:**
- Always ask a clarifying question after an objection
- Never argue — acknowledge, then redirect
- Use their name at least once in each response
- Keep responses under 30 seconds
- Log objections in LEAD-TRACKER.csv notes column
- Update status after objection handling
