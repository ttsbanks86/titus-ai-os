# Call Script — Initial Cold Call

## Opening (10 seconds)

"Hi {{First Name}}, this is Titus Banks. I sent you a quick email about {{Business Name}}'s Google reviews. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**If yes:** Continue to pitch.
**If no:** "No problem. When would be a better time to call back?"
**If voicemail:** Leave voicemail (see voicemail script).

---

## Pitch (30 seconds)

"I was looking at {{Business Name}} on Google and noticed you have {{Review Count}} reviews. That's good, but I see a few easy wins:

1. You haven't responded to {{Number}} of your last reviews
2. You don't have a system for requesting reviews from happy customers
3. Your website doesn't have a booking form

I help businesses like yours fix all three of these in about 3 days. The result is more reviews, more trust, and more customers.

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call where I walk you through exactly what I'd do for {{Business Name}}?"

---

## Objection Handling

**"We already have a guy for that."**
"No problem. Most of my clients also have a marketing person or agency. I'm not trying to replace anyone. I just focus on one thing: getting you more Google reviews and following up with missed leads. Would it help to get a second opinion on that specific area?"

**"We're too busy right now."**
"I get it. That's actually why this works — it runs in the background while you're on job sites. Most businesses see results in 2-3 weeks with about 30 minutes of setup. Would 15 minutes next week work better?"

**"How much does it cost?"**
"It depends on what you need. I have a basic package starting at $250 setup and $97/month, and a full package at $500 setup and $197/month. But let me first understand what you need, then I can recommend the right fit. Can I ask you a few questions?"

**"I need to think about it."**
"Totally understand. What specifically do you want to think about? Is it the price, the timing, or something else?"

**"Send me more information."**
"Happy to. What's the best email address? I'll send you a quick overview of how this works for businesses like yours. Can I follow up in a day or two to see what you think?"

---

## Close

**If interested:** "Great. Would Tuesday or Thursday work for a quick 15-minute call? I can walk you through exactly what I'd do for {{Business Name}}."

**If not interested:** "No problem. Thanks for your time, {{First Name}}. If you ever want to revisit this, just give me a call."

**If voicemail:** "Hi {{First Name}}, this is Titus Banks. I sent you a quick email about {{Business Name}}'s Google reviews. I noticed a few easy wins that could help you get more customers. Give me a call back at {{Your Phone Number}} when you get a chance. Thanks."

---

**Notes:**
- Always ask for the meeting before hanging up
- Use their name at least 2 times during the call
- Reference a specific issue from the mini-audit
- Keep the call under 5 minutes
- Log the call in LEAD-TRACKER.csv immediately after
- Update status: "Called — Interested" or "Called — Not Interested"

**Template Variables:**
- `{{Business Name}}` — Business name from Google Maps
- `{{First Name}}` — Contact name if available, otherwise use "there"
- `{{Review Count}}` — Current Google review count
- `{{Number}}` — Number of unresponded reviews
- `{{Your Phone Number}}` — Titus's phone number
