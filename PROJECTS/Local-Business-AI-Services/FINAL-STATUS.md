## Key Features

### Business Model
- **First Niche:** Home service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, electricians)
- **First Package:** Review Growth System ($250-500 setup, $97-197/mo)
- **Tool Stack:** $0/month MVP using existing free tools
- **Revenue Target:** $700 month 1, $2,100 month 2, $4,200 month 3

### Sales Process
- **Lead Research:** Google Maps, Yelp, Facebook, LinkedIn, directories
- **Cold Outreach:** Email, phone, LinkedIn templates
- **Sales Call:** Scripts, objection handling, appointment setting
- **Closing:** Proposals, contracts, payment collection

### Delivery Process
- **Review Growth System:** 2-3 day setup, 30 min/month reporting
- **Lead Follow-Up System:** 3-5 day setup, 1 hour/week reporting
- **AI Content System:** 5-7 day setup, 2-3 hours/month reporting

### Measurement
- **Daily:** Leads, emails, calls, replies
- **Weekly:** Conversion rates, meetings, proposals
- **Monthly:** Revenue, clients, retention
- **KPIs:** Reply rate 20%, meeting rate 5%, close rate 30%
