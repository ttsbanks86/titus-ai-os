## General Delivery Rules

### Communication
- Respond to client emails within 24 hours
- Send daily updates during setup
- Send weekly updates during ongoing service
- Always be professional and helpful

### Quality
- Double-check all work before delivery
- Test all links and forms
- Proofread all content
- Ensure brand consistency

### Documentation
- Log all work in client folder
- Update CRM after every client interaction
- Save all templates and assets
- Maintain version history

### Time Management
- Set realistic deadlines
- Deliver on time or early
- Communicate delays immediately
- Track time for each client
