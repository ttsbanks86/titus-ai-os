## 3. Recommended Business Model

### First Niche: Home Service Businesses (Plumbers, HVAC, Electricians)

**Why this niche first:**

1. **High customer value.** One plumbing job = $200-2,000. One HVAC install = $3,000-8,000. One missed lead = significant revenue loss. The ROI of our service is immediately obvious.

2. **Frequent lead leaks.** These businesses are on job sites all day. They miss calls. They don't text back. They don't follow up with website forms. This is the exact problem we solve.

3. **Weak online presence.** Most don't have a professional website, don't respond to Google reviews, and don't post on social media. Easy wins.

4. **Easy to reach.** Google Maps + Yelp + Facebook gives us their name, phone, website, reviews, and hours. No scraping needed.

5. **Easy to audit.** Call their number (test if they answer). Check their website (is there a booking form?). Read their Google reviews (do they respond?). 10-minute audit.

6. **High ROI for them.** If we help them book one extra job per month ($500+), our $197-397/month service pays for itself 1.5-2.5x over.

### Second Niche (after validation): Dentists and Med Spas

These businesses have higher margins, more predictable schedules, and更大的 marketing budgets. They're a natural second target.

### Third Niche (after validation): Cleaning Companies and Landscapers

Volume businesses with high turnover. They need consistent lead flow and review generation.

## 6. Compliance Rules

### Email Outreach (CAN-SPAM)
- Include physical address in every email
- Include unsubscribe link
- Don't use deceptive subject lines
- Honor unsubscribe requests within 10 days
- Don't email harvested addresses at scale (use targeted, personalized outreach)

### SMS Outreach (TCPA)
- Don't cold text businesses without consent
- Email and phone are safer first touches
- If texting clients' customers, get written consent
- Include opt-out in every text
- Don't text before 8am or after 9pm local time

### Google Reviews (Google ToS)
- Don't offer payment for reviews
- Don't review your own clients
- Don't post fake reviews
- DO make it easy for real customers to leave reviews
- DO respond to all reviews professionally

### Cold Outreach Best Practices
- Personalize every email (mention their business name, specific issue)
- Keep emails under 200 words
- Send from a professional email address
- Don't send more than 20 emails per day from a new account
- Warm up your email domain before scaling
- Track opens and replies
