## Overview

This document outlines the compliance rules and regulations for Review & Lead Recovery System. Follow these rules strictly to avoid legal issues.

## CAN-SPAM Act

### Requirements

1. **Physical Address** — Include your physical address in every commercial email.
2. **Unsubscribe Link** — Include a clear unsubscribe link in every email.
3. **No Deceptive Subjects** — Don't use misleading subject lines.
4. **Honor Unsubscribes** — Process unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.
5. **Identify as Ad** — Clearly identify the email as an advertisement.

### Best Practices

- Use a professional email address (e.g., yourname@yourdomain.com)
- Personalize every email (mention business name)
- Keep emails under 200 words
- Don't send more than 20 emails per day from a new account
- Warm up your email domain before scaling
- Track opens and replies

### Example Compliance Email

```
Subject: Quick question about ABC Plumbing's Google reviews

Hi there,

I was looking at ABC Plumbing on Google and noticed you have 45 reviews with a 4.5 star rating. That's solid.

I help local service businesses get more Google reviews and follow up with missed leads. Most businesses I work with see 3-5 new reviews per week once we set up a simple system.

Would you be open to a quick 10-minute chat about how this works for businesses like ABC Plumbing?

No pressure — just want to see if it's a fit.

Best,
Titus Banks
(206) 555-0101
titus@yourdomain.com

P.S. I put together a quick audit of ABC Plumbing's online presence. Happy to send it over if you're interested.

Unsubscribe | Manage Preferences
123 Main St, Seattle, WA 98101
```

## TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)

### Requirements

1. **No Cold Texting** — Don't text businesses without prior consent.
2. **Time Restrictions** — Don't call before 8am or after 9pm local time.
3. **Do Not Call List** — Check the Do Not Call registry before calling.
4. **Opt-Out** — Include opt-out instructions in every text message.
5. **Consent** — Get written consent before sending automated texts.

### Best Practices

- Email and phone are safer first touches than text
- If texting clients' customers, get written consent from the business
- Include opt-out in every text (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe")
- Don't call before 8am or after 9pm
- Keep records of consent

### Example Compliance Text

```
Hi [Name], this is Titus Banks. I help businesses like yours get more Google reviews. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call? Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
```

## Google Reviews (Google ToS)

### Requirements

1. **No Payment for Reviews** — Don't offer money or incentives for reviews.
2. **No Fake Reviews** — Don't post fake reviews or pay for reviews.
3. **No Self-Reviews** — Don't review your own clients' businesses.
4. **No Review Gating** — Don't selectively ask happy customers for reviews while hiding negative feedback.

### Best Practices

- Make it easy for real customers to leave reviews
- Respond to all reviews professionally
- Use a feedback form to capture negative reviews before they go public
- Never offer incentives for reviews
- Never post fake reviews

### Example Compliance Review Request

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for choosing ABC Plumbing! We hope we did a great job for you.

If you have a moment, we'd really appreciate a review on Google. It helps other customers find us and lets us know how we're doing.

[Review Link]

If there's anything we could have done better, please let us know directly using our feedback form: [Feedback Form Link]

Thank you for your business!

Best,
ABC Plumbing
```

## Cold Outreach Best Practices

### Email Outreach

1. **Personalize** — Mention business name and specific issue.
2. **Keep It Short** — Under 200 words.
3. **Professional Email** — Use your business email address.
4. **Limit Volume** — Don't send more than 20 emails per day from a new account.
5. **Warm Up** — Warm up your email domain before scaling.
6. **Track** — Track opens and replies.
7. **Follow Up** — Follow up within 48 hours if no response.

### Phone Outreach

1. **Time It Right** — Call during business hours (9am-5pm).
2. **Be Brief** — Keep calls under 5 minutes.
3. **Be Professional** — Use a professional tone.
4. **Log Everything** — Log calls in LEAD-TRACKER.csv.
5. **Follow Up** — Follow up with an email after every call.

### LinkedIn Outreach

1. **Connect First** — Send a connection request with a personalized note.
2. **Engage** — Like and comment on their posts before messaging.
3. **Message** — Send a personalized message after they accept.
4. **Don't Spam** — Don't send more than 5 messages per day.
5. **Be Helpful** — Offer value before asking for anything.

## Client Data Protection

### Requirements

1. **Confidentiality** — Keep client data confidential.
2. **Access Control** — Only access client data when necessary.
3. **Data Storage** — Store client data securely.
4. **Data Retention** — Delete client data when no longer needed.
5. **Breach Notification** — Notify clients immediately if data is compromised.

### Best Practices

- Use strong passwords for all accounts
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Don't share client data with third parties
- Don't use client data for personal purposes
- Regularly back up client data
- Delete client data when the relationship ends

## Business Registration

### Requirements

1. **Business License** — Obtain a business license from your city/county.
2. **State Registration** — Register your business with the state.
3. **EIN** — Obtain an Employer Identification Number from the IRS.
4. **Bank Account** — Open a business bank account.
5. **Insurance** — Obtain general liability insurance.

### Best Practices

- Consult with a lawyer to ensure compliance
- Consult with an accountant for tax planning
- Keep accurate financial records
- Pay estimated taxes quarterly
- File annual reports on time

---

## Tax Compliance

### Requirements

1. **Income Tax** — Report all income on your tax return.
2. **Self-Employment Tax** — Pay self-employment tax (Social Security + Medicare).
3. **Sales Tax** — Collect and remit sales tax if required.
4. **Estimated Taxes** — Pay estimated taxes quarterly.
5. **Record Keeping** — Keep accurate financial records.

### Best Practices

- Consult with an accountant for tax planning
- Use accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks, Wave)
- Keep receipts for all business expenses
- Set aside 25-30% of income for taxes
- File taxes on time
