# 14-Day Launch Plan — Review & Lead Recovery System

**Start date:** [Date you begin]
**Goal:** 1 paying client within 14 days
**Daily time commitment:** 2-3 hours

---

## Week 1: Setup + First Outreach

### Day 1 — Foundation Setup (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Create Gmail account for the business (or use existing professional email)
- [ ] Set up Google Sheets CRM (copy from `_data/LEAD-TRACKER.csv`)
- [ ] Set up Calendly free tier (1 event type: "15-Minute Business Review")
- [ ] Test Calendly booking link (book yourself, then cancel)

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Set up MailerLite free account
- [ ] Create first MailerLite form (for prospect lead capture)
- [ ] Set up Google Voice business number (optional)
- [ ] Record 60-second Loom intro video (who you are, what you do, who you help)

**End of day check:** Can a prospect book a call with you? Can they fill out a form? Can you send an email? If yes to all three, you're ready.

---

### Day 2 — Lead Research: First 20 Leads (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Open Google Maps
- [ ] Search: "plumber [your city]" or "HVAC [your city]" or "electrician [your city]"
- [ ] Open each business listing
- [ ] Collect for each: Business name, phone, website, Google rating, review count, address
- [ ] Add first 10 leads to Google Sheets CRM

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Continue lead research (10 more leads)
- [ ] For each lead, note one "problem noticed" (few reviews, no website, bad photos, no posts)
- [ ] Score each lead using the lead scoring system (see README.md)
- [ ] Prioritize top 5 leads (highest score)

**End of day check:** 20 leads in CRM. Top 5 identified. Ready to audit.

---

### Day 3 — Mini-Audits + More Leads (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Create 3 mini-audits for top prospects (use `MINI-AUDIT-TEMPLATE.md`)
- [ ] For each audit:
  - Check Google review count and rating
  - Check if they respond to reviews
  - Check their website (is there a booking form? contact form? phone number visible?)
  - Check their social media (when was last post?)
  - Note one specific problem you can fix
  - Recommend one package

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Add 20 more leads to CRM (total: 40)
- [ ] Create 2 more mini-audits (total: 5)
- [ ] Prepare first outreach email (customize `cold-email-1.md`)

**End of day check:** 40 leads. 5 mini-audits. First email ready to send.

---

### Day 4 — First Outreach (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Send 20 personalized cold emails (use `cold-email-1.md`)
- [ ] Each email must reference the prospect's business name and one specific issue
- [ ] Track in CRM: "Email Sent" date, "Contact Status" = "Email 1 Sent"

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Send 5 more emails (total: 25 sent)
- [ ] Make 5 phone calls to top prospects (use `call-script.md`)
- [ ] Leave voicemails for those who don't answer (use `voicemail-script.md`)
- [ ] Update CRM with call notes

**End of day check:** 25 emails sent. 5 calls made. 5 voicemails left.

---

### Day 5 — Follow-Up + More Leads (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Add 20 more leads to CRM (total: 60)
- [ ] Send follow-up emails to Day 4 prospects who didn't reply (use `follow-up-email-1.md`)
- [ ] Make 5 more phone calls

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Create 3 more mini-audits (total: 8)
- [ ] Send review audit emails to 5 prospects (use `review-audit-email.md`)
- [ ] Update CRM with all activity

**End of day check:** 60 leads. 8 mini-audits. 30+ emails sent. 10 calls made.

---

### Day 6 — Intensive Outreach (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Send 20 more cold emails (total: 50+ sent)
- [ ] Follow up with Day 4 prospects (3 days after first email)
- [ ] Make 5 phone calls

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Add 20 more leads (total: 80)
- [ ] Send lead follow-up audit emails to 5 prospects (use `lead-followup-audit-email.md`)
- [ ] Update CRM

**End of day check:** 80 leads. 50+ emails sent. 15 calls made.

---

### Day 7 — Review + Adjust (1-2 hours)

**Morning (60 min):**
- [ ] Review CRM: How many replies? How many calls answered? How many meetings booked?
- [ ] Analyze: Which email subject lines got opens? Which messages got replies?
- [ ] Identify: Which niche is responding best? Which package are they interested in?

**Afternoon (60 min):**
- [ ] Adjust email templates based on responses
- [ ] Update lead scoring if needed
- [ ] Plan Week 2 based on data

**End of day check:** Week 1 data analyzed. Week 2 plan ready.

---

---

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Add 20 more leads (if needed to stay at 100+ active)
- [ ] Create 3 more mini-audits (total: 14)

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Send 20 cold emails
- [ ] Follow up with all prospects from Week 1 who haven't responded (use `follow-up-email-2.md`)

**End of day check:** 100+ leads. 14 mini-audits. 110+ emails sent.

---

### Day 11 — Intensive Follow-Up (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Send 20 cold emails
- [ ] Follow up with all prospects who replied but didn't book
- [ ] Make 5 phone calls

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Send personalized messages to hot leads
- [ ] Offer free mini-audits to warm leads
- [ ] Update CRM

**End of day check:** 130+ emails sent. 25+ calls made.

---

### Day 12 — Book Calls (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Focus on converting replies to booked calls
- [ ] Send Calendly links to interested prospects
- [ ] Confirm any scheduled calls for today/tomorrow

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Make 5 phone calls to warm leads
- [ ] Follow up with all prospects who showed interest
- [ ] Update CRM

**End of day check:** 1-3 calls booked. Pipeline moving.

---

### Day 13 — Proposals + Close (2-3 hours)

**Morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Conduct any scheduled sales calls
- [ ] Send proposals to interested prospects (use `proposal-template.md`)
- [ ] Follow up on any pending proposals

**Afternoon (90 min):**
- [ ] Make 5 phone calls
- [ ] Send personalized messages to hot leads
- [ ] Update CRM with proposal status

**End of day check:** 1-2 proposals sent. 1 deal potentially closed.

---

### Day 14 — Review + Plan (1-2 hours)

**Morning (60 min):**
- [ ] Full CRM review: leads, emails, calls, replies, meetings, proposals, deals
- [ ] Calculate: reply rate, meeting rate, close rate
- [ ] Identify: what worked, what didn't, what to change

**Afternoon (60 min):**
- [ ] Plan Week 3 based on data
- [ ] Update outreach templates based on learnings
- [ ] Set targets for Week 3

**End of day check:** Week 1-2 data complete. Week 3 plan ready. 1+ paying client.

## Weekly Review (Every Sunday, 30 minutes)

1. Pull CRM data. Count: leads, emails, calls, replies, meetings, proposals, deals.
2. Calculate: reply rate, meeting rate, close rate.
3. Identify the top 3 things that worked.
4. Identify the top 3 things that didn't work.
5. Decide: what to keep, what to change, what to try next week.
6. Set Week 3 targets.
