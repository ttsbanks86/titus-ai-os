## Question 1

**According to the book, if not committees, policies, and majority rule, then what?**

- A. Committees
- B. Policies
- **C. A new wineskin** ✅
- D. Majority rule

**Answer:** #2 — a new wineskin

**Page Reference:** 108–110 (Rethinking Structure chapter)

**Context:** White critiques committees, policies, and majority rule as the traditional bureaucratic models churches default to, then introduces the concept of "new wineskins" (from Jesus' teaching in Luke 5:36–38) as the biblical alternative — flexible structures that can adapt to what God is doing.

## Question 2

**What is the example of "teams" in ministry used in the book?**

- **A. Nehemiah's strategy for rebuilding the wall of Jerusalem** ✅
- B. The early church in Acts
- C. The disciples' ministry in the Gospels
- D. Paul's missionary journeys

**Answer:** #1 — Nehemiah's strategy for rebuilding the wall of Jerusalem

**Page Reference:** 108–110 (Rethinking Structure chapter)

**Context:** White uses Nehemiah's approach of organizing the people into teams, each responsible for a section of the wall, as a biblical model for team-based ministry structure.

## Question 3

**Whose story is used in this chapter to illustrate how bureaucracy can inhibit decision-making in ministry?**

- A. William Carey
- B. Billy Graham
- **C. Mother Teresa** ✅
- D. Martin Luther

**Answer:** #3 — Mother Teresa

**Page Reference:** 109–110

**Quote (p. 110):** *"The story involving Mother Teresa demonstrates how important the process of decision making is to ministry and how inhibiting bureaucracy can become."*

**Context:** The full passage (pp. 109–110) tells how Mother Teresa's Missionaries of Charity spent a year and a half navigating New York City bureaucracy to get approval for a homeless shelter — traveling from hearing room to hearing room — then eventually gave up, deciding the money could be better used "for soup and sandwiches." White uses this to illustrate how structure and bureaucracy, when excessive, inhibit effective ministry.

## Question 4

**Which of the following is NOT affected by structure?**

- A. Morale
- B. Effectiveness
- C. Unity
- **D. Profitability** ✅

**Answer:** #4 — profitability

**Page Reference:** ~108 (Rethinking Structure chapter)

**Context:** White argues that structure affects morale, effectiveness, and unity — but not profitability, as the church is not a for-profit enterprise. This highlights how church structure must be evaluated by different criteria than business structure.
