## 🟦 Q1 — George Barna's Analogy for the Church

> According to George Barna, what analogy does he use to describe the church?
> * A bird soaring in the sky
> * A fish swimming in the ocean
> * A frog in a kettle of water
> * A lion ruling over the jungle

**Answer: A frog in a kettle of water**

**Source:** p. 158 — "George Barna likens the church to a frog in a kettle of water. He notes that if you place a frog in a kettle of water and then begin to raise the water's temperature slowly, the frog will continue to remain in the water, even to the boiling point. Because the temperature is increased gradually, the frog does not sense it is in danger and stays in the water until its death. 'Like the frog,' writes Barna, 'we are faced with the very real possibility of dying because of our unresponsiveness to the changing world around us.'"

## 🟦 Q2 — Change Does Not Have to Mean

> Change does not have to mean ____.
> * overhaul
> * compromise
> * settling
> * betrayal

**Answer: compromise**

**Source:** p. 159 — "At the same time, change does not have to mean compromise. Interestingly, even such a dispassionate observer as the Atlantic Monthly noted that innovative churches 'may be market-driven, culturally sensitive, and cutting-edge, but this does not make [them] "progressive" or "liberal" on the fundamentals.'"

## 🟦 Q3 — Secondary Theme: Church Must Rethink Its ___

> There has been a secondary theme to this book that has been somewhat more reserved in its presentation. Along with a rethinking of processes, the church must rethink its ____.
> * investment
> * attitude
> * strategy
> * mission

**Answer: attitude**

**Source:** p. 159 — "But there has been a secondary theme to this book that has been somewhat more reserved in its presentation. Along with a rethinking of processes, the church must rethink its attitude."

## 🟦 Q4 — The Church's Mission

> You are preaching a sermon on the one most important thing THE CHURCH must fulfill; it's mission. What would it be?
> * The Good Commandment
> * The Beatitudes
> * The Golden Rule
> * The Great Commission

**Answer: The Great Commission**

**Source:** p. 160 — "The purpose of the church is to fulfill the Great Commission. We do not grow in Christ for our own sake, but for the sake of the cause."

Also p. 157 — "The church must rethink its current processes in order to determine if it will effectively fulfill the Great Commission."
