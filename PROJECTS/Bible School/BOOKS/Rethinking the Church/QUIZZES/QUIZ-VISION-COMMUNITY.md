# Quiz — Rethinking Vision & Community
**Book:** *Rethinking the Church* by James Emery White  
**Chapter:** Rethinking Vision / Rethinking Community  
**Date Answered:** June 3, 2026

---

## Q1 — Vision Definition
> According to James Emery White, a vision is defined as...

**Answer:** An image, a picture, of what we are trying to do or become  
**Source:** p. 137 — "A vision is an image, a picture, of what we are trying to do or become."

---

## Q2 — Marks of Community (Acts 2:45)
> According to the author, which of the following is NOT one of the marks of community found in Acts 2:45?

**Context:** The third mark of real community is "Serve and Be Served," which is explicitly tied to Acts 2:45 ("selling their possessions and goods, they gave to anyone as he had need"). The three marks of authentic community are:
1. Know and Be Known
2. *(Cultivation of a community of safe people)*
3. Serve and Be Served (Acts 2:45)

**Answer:** The marks NOT found in Acts 2:45 are the first two marks (Know and Be Known / safe people / authenticity). The mark *from* Acts 2:45 is "Serve and Be Served."

## Q3 — Sign in the Mirror Associated with the Soul
> What sign in the mirror is associated with the soul?

**Answer:** An absence of authenticity  
**Source:** p. 124 — The first sign is "an absence of authenticity." In this section, the author discusses the Greek word *psuche* (meaning "soul") from 1 Thessalonians 2:8, where Paul writes that he shared not only the gospel but his very life/soul with the believers. The absence of authenticity (wearing masks, hiding true self) is the sign associated with the soul.

The three signs in the mirror are:
1. An absence of authenticity ✦ ← (associated with psuche/soul)
2. The presence of broken relationships
3. A spirit of exclusion

## Q4 — Five Things That Form the Basis for Uniting as a Church
> According to the author, five specific things form the basis for uniting as a church. List them in order from most important to least.

**Answer:**  
1. Faith in Christ  
2. Values and beliefs  
3. Purpose and mission  
4. Strategy for achieving that purpose and mission  
5. Structure  

**Source:** p. 135 — "What difference does this make to community? You cannot be unified as a community unless you have defined what that unity will be based on. Five things usually form the basis for uniting together as a church: 1. faith in Christ, 2. values and beliefs, 3. purpose and mission, 4. strategy for achieving that purpose and mission, 5. structure."
