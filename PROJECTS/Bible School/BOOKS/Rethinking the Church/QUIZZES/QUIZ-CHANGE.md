# Quiz — Rethinking Change
**Book:** *Rethinking the Church* by James Emery White  
**Chapter:** From Rethinking to Change  
**Date Answered:** June 3, 2026

---

## 🟦 Q1 — NOT a Characteristic of an Agent of Change

> Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of an agent of change?
> * charisma
> * courage
> * determination

**Answer:** **Charisma** is NOT a characteristic of an agent of change.

**Source:** pp. 154-155 — "Character Traits of Change Agents" lists two traits:
1. **Courage** — "The first is courage."
2. **Determination** — "The second character trait that change demands is determination."

Charisma is not mentioned among the traits.

## 🟦 Q2 — First Trait of Change Agents

> Which of the following character traits is considered the first trait of change agents?
> * Humility
> * Courage
> * Empathy
> * Resilience

**Answer:** **Courage**

**Source:** p. 154 — "The first is courage."

## 🟦 Q3 — Step Where You Show Change Would Be a Tragedy

> If you wanted to bring people to the point of understanding that it would be a tragedy if change didn't happen, you would do it in what step of change?
> * Establish a sense of urgency
> * Develop and cast a compelling vision
> * Begin implementation
> * Give progress reports

**Answer:** **Establish a sense of urgency**

**Source:** p. 150 — "1. Establish a Sense of Urgency" — *"A perceived problem or need must be generating a certain amount of emotional energy... A sense of urgency must be conveyed. The Bible reminds us that we are transformed through the renewing of our mind (Rom. 12:2). So whatever the change may be, be sure to convey what the stakes are and why the change is so important."*

Bringing people to understand it would be a tragedy if change didn't happen is about conveying the stakes — this is the core of establishing a sense of urgency.

## 🟦 Q4 — Five Groups in Effective Change (In Order)

> Put the five groups involved in effective change in the order given in the book.
> * late adopters
> * innovators
> * resistant
> * early adopters
> * opinion leaders

**Answer** (in order):

| # | Group | % of Church |
|---|-------|:-----------:|
| **1** | **Innovators** | ~2% |
| **2** | **Opinion leaders** | ~14% |
| **3** | **Early adopters** | ~33% |
| **4** | **Late adopters** | ~33% |
| **5** | **Resistant** | ~16% |

**Source:** pp. 146-147 — *"The Five Groups—And What to Do with Them"*
- "First, there are the **innovators**" (2%)
- "Then you have the **opinion leaders**" (14%)
- Together = "your community of leaders"
- "**Early adopters**" (~one-third)
- "Then come the **late adopters**" (~one-third)
- "The last group is the **resistant**" (16%)

The change flows like dominoes: Innovators → Opinion Leaders → Early Adopters → Late Adopters → Resistant.
