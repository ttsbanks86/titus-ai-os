## Current Situation

BrightCare Home Services is a fictional home-care provider that manages caregiver shifts, client assignments, and service documentation through informal, decentralized processes. The current operational model is characterized by:

- Fragmented communication across scheduling, care coordination, and client services
- Manual tracking of shift status, attendance, and documentation completion
- No centralized system for identifying or escalating service issues
- No operational dashboard or KPI reporting
- Reactive issue resolution rather than proactive prevention
- High administrative overhead relative to organizational size

---

## Need for Change

The current operational model creates risks that increase with organizational complexity:

- **Service reliability risk:** Missed and late shifts directly affect client care
- **Compliance risk:** Incomplete documentation creates audit exposure
- **Client retention risk:** Service failures without structured follow-up reduce loyalty
- **Staff efficiency risk:** Manual processes consume time that could serve more clients
- **Growth risk:** The current model does not scale without proportional administrative cost increase
- **Decision-making risk:** No data exists to identify root causes or measure improvement

---

## Proposed High-Level Solution

The proposed solution is a structured BA analysis framework with a supporting demonstration tool (BA Compass) that:

1. Documents current-state operational processes
2. Identifies pain points, gaps, and root causes
3. Defines business, functional, and nonfunctional requirements
4. Creates traceable user stories and acceptance criteria
5. Designs future-state process improvements
6. Defines KPIs and success measures
7. Produces professional BA deliverables (BRD, traceability matrix, risk register)
8. Demonstrates the full BA lifecycle in a recruiter-friendly format
