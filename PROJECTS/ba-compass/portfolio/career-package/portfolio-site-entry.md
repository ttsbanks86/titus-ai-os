## Medium Description (for project detail page)

BA Compass is a comprehensive BA portfolio project built around a fictional home-care agency, BrightCare Home Services. The project covers the complete BA lifecycle: problem definition, stakeholder analysis with power/interest mapping, a formal Business Requirements Document (BRD) with 24 requirements, MoSCoW prioritization, 12 operational KPIs with an interactive dashboard, 11 assessed risks with mitigation strategies, and a fully traced requirements matrix. All requirements are implemented in a Next.js 15 application with 76 unit tests and 14 end-to-end tests, making the entire portfolio interactive and explorable.

All data is synthetic. BrightCare Home Services is fictional. AI tools were used to accelerate application development; all BA analysis and methodology is my own work.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Business Requirements Documentation, Stakeholder Analysis, Requirements Prioritization, KPI Definition, Risk Management, Process Improvement, Traceability Management, Quality Assurance
