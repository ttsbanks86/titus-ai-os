# Handshake Portfolio Entry — BA Compass

---

## Overview

BA Compass is a Business Analyst portfolio project built around a fictional home-care agency called BrightCare Home Services. The project simulates a real-world BA engagement: a growing agency with fragmented scheduling, paper-based care documentation, and delayed billing needs a structured requirements analysis and a technology solution. As the sole Business Analyst, I managed the full BA lifecycle from problem definition through requirements documentation, stakeholder analysis, KPI design, risk assessment, and application delivery.

---

## Challenge

BrightCare Home Services (fictional) operates in a competitive home-care market. Growth has outpaced their operational processes. Scheduling conflicts cause missed visits. Care documentation is handwritten and often submitted days late. Billing cycles drag because paper records must be manually entered into the billing system. Leadership has inconsistent visibility into operational performance. The organization needs a clear, prioritized set of requirements and a system that addresses the most critical pain points first.

---

## Approach

- **Problem Definition:** Analyzed the business context to define a clear problem statement with measurable success criteria.

- **Stakeholder Analysis:** Identified 14 stakeholders across 4 domains. Mapped them to a power/interest grid and created an engagement strategy for each group.

- **Requirements Elicitation:** Conducted a structured elicitation process resulting in 16 functional and 8 non-functional requirements. Used MoSCoW prioritization to align scope with business value.

- **Process Analysis:** Documented as-is workflows for scheduling, care documentation, and billing. Designed future-state processes targeting 50-60% reduction in manual touchpoints.

- **KPI Definition:** Defined 12 operational KPIs with target values, measurement methodology, and frequency. Built an interactive dashboard for ongoing monitoring.

- **Risk Assessment:** Facilitated a structured risk identification workshop. Created a risk register with 11 risks, probability/impact scores, and 9 mitigation strategies.

- **Requirements Traceability:** Built a complete traceability matrix linking each requirement to test cases and application features, ensuring 100% coverage.

- **Solution Delivery:** Worked with development tools to build a Next.js 15 application that implements all requirements, verified through 76 unit tests and 14 end-to-end tests.
