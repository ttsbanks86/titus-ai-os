## 2-Minute Version

BA Compass is a comprehensive Business Analyst portfolio project that walks through a complete BA engagement from problem definition to solution delivery.

I started with a fictional home-care agency called BrightCare Home Services. The business problem was clear: the agency had grown quickly, but its processes hadn't kept up. Scheduling was handled through a mix of phone calls and spreadsheets, leading to conflicts and missed visits. Care documentation was paper-based and often submitted days late. Billing cycles were slow because paper records needed manual data entry. Leadership had no consistent visibility into operational performance.

My first step was defining the problem statement and success criteria. Then I identified 14 stakeholders and mapped them to a power/interest grid, which gave me a clear picture of who needed to be engaged and how. I documented the current-state processes for scheduling, care documentation, and billing, identifying 7 specific pain points.

For requirements, I authored a Business Requirements Document with 16 functional and 8 non-functional requirements, each with detailed descriptions and acceptance criteria. I used MoSCoW prioritization to categorize every requirement and reduced the initial scope by about 20% while keeping all high-priority clinical and billing capabilities intact. Every requirement was then entered into a traceability matrix linked to test cases and application features for full coverage.

I defined 12 KPIs across four domains: scheduling efficiency, clinical quality, financial performance, and workforce management. Each KPI has a clear definition, measurement methodology, target value, and data source. I built an interactive dashboard using Recharts that displays trend data, target comparisons, and status indicators.

Risk assessment was another key phase. I identified 11 risks, scored them by probability and impact, developed 9 mitigation strategies, and created contingency plans for the highest-severity items.

The final deliverable is a full-stack Next.js 15 application with TypeScript strict mode, 16 routes, 19 static pages, 76 unit tests, and 14 end-to-end tests. It includes a BRD viewer, requirements editor, KPI dashboard, traceability matrix, risk register, executive summary, and a recruiter tour mode. Every feature traces back to a specific business requirement.

All data in this project is synthetic. BrightCare is a fictional company. I used AI tools to help with application development, but every BA decision, analysis, methodology choice, and deliverable is my own work. This project proves I can do the job.
