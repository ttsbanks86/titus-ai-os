BA Compass is a comprehensive Business Analyst portfolio project that demonstrates the full BA lifecycle from problem definition through solution delivery. The project centers on BrightCare Home Services, a fictional home-care agency with operational challenges including fragmented scheduling, paper-based care documentation, and delayed billing cycles.

As the sole Business Analyst on this project, I managed the complete BA workflow. I began by defining the business problem with measurable success criteria and conducting a stakeholder analysis that identified 14 stakeholders across operations, clinical, finance, and technology domains, each mapped to a power/interest grid with tailored engagement strategies. I authored a formal Business Requirements Document containing 16 functional and 8 non-functional requirements, each with detailed acceptance criteria, and led a MoSCoW prioritization exercise that aligned scope with business value while maintaining stakeholder consensus.

I defined 12 operational KPIs spanning scheduling efficiency, clinical quality, financial performance, and workforce management, each with documented measurement methodology and target values, and designed an interactive dashboard to visualize synthetic trend data. I facilitated a structured risk assessment that identified 11 project risks with probability/impact scoring and developed 9 mitigation strategies documented in a risk register. Finally, I built a complete requirements traceability matrix linking every requirement to test cases and application features, achieving 100% coverage verified through 76 unit tests and 14 end-to-end tests in a TypeScript strict mode application.

All data is synthetic and BrightCare Home Services is fictional. AI tools were used to accelerate application development; all BA analysis and methodology is my own work. The project demonstrates my ability to manage the full BA lifecycle, produce professional deliverables, and bridge business needs with technical implementation.
