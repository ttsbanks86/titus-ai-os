## Project Entry

**Project Name:** BA Compass — BrightCare Home Services Portfolio

**Role:** Business Analyst (sole contributor)

**Date:** 2026

**Description:**

A comprehensive Business Analyst portfolio project built from the ground up to demonstrate the full BA lifecycle. Using a fictional home-care agency (BrightCare Home Services), I defined business problems, analyzed stakeholders, documented requirements, designed KPIs, assessed risks, and built a working web application that serves as an interactive portfolio artifact.

The project covers the complete BA workflow: stakeholder analysis with power/interest mapping, MoSCoW prioritization, a formal Business Requirements Document (BRD) with 24 requirements, a requirements traceability matrix, a KPI dashboard with 12 operational metrics, risk register with 11 identified risks and mitigation strategies, current-state and future-state process analysis, and a feature-complete web application demonstrating each requirement in action.

All data is synthetic. BrightCare Home Services is a fictional company. This project is a portfolio simulation designed to demonstrate BA competencies.

**Top Skills:** Business Requirements Documentation, Stakeholder Analysis, Requirements Prioritization (MoSCoW), KPI Definition, Risk Management, Process Improvement, Traceability Matrix, User Acceptance Testing Criteria

---

## 5 Strongest Accomplishments

1. **End-to-End BA Lifecycle:** Delivered every BA artifact from problem statement through requirements documentation, traceability, risk analysis, KPI design, and working software demonstration covering 16 business routes and 19 static pages.

2. **Structured Requirements Management:** Authored a BRD with 16 functional and 8 non-functional requirements, each with detailed descriptions, acceptance criteria, and bidirectional traceability to test cases and application features. Zero orphan requirements.

3. **Stakeholder-Driven Prioritization:** Analyzed 14 stakeholders across operations, clinical, finance, and technology domains. Used power/interest mapping and MoSCoW prioritization to reduce initial scope by 20% while protecting all high-value clinical and billing workflows.

4. **KPI Dashboard Design:** Defined 12 operational KPIs spanning scheduling efficiency, caregiver retention, revenue cycle, and clinical compliance. Built an interactive Recharts dashboard that visualizes synthetic trend data with target comparisons and status indicators.

5. **Quality-First Delivery:** Achieved 76 unit tests and 14 end-to-end tests with TypeScript strict mode, ensuring that all requirements are verifiable and the application meets specified quality standards.
