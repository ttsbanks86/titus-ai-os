# CCPA / Data Broker Opt-Out Letter Template

**Use this template to opt out of data broker databases and other commercial data sellers.** The strongest legal basis is the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), which grants California residents specific opt-out rights. Several other states have similar laws (Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, etc.). For non-residents, many brokers honor opt-out requests as a matter of policy even without a legal obligation.

**This is a separate workflow from credit disputes.** It removes your personal information from websites and databases that SELL your data — not from your credit report. Some sites (people search engines, marketing data brokers) are legally required to honor these requests in CA. Others will honor them as a matter of policy.

---

## CCPA Opt-Out Letter (For California Residents)

```
[Date]

[Company Name]
[Privacy Office / Compliance Department]
[Company Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

Re: California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) — Right to Delete and Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing

To Whom It May Concern:

I am a California resident exercising my rights under the California
Consumer Privacy Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.) and the
California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Please treat this letter as a formal request for the following:

1. RIGHT TO KNOW (CCPA § 1798.100, § 1798.110, § 1798.115)
   Please provide me with copies of all personal information you have
   collected about me, including:
   - Identifiers (name, alias, postal address, unique personal identifiers,
     online identifiers, IP address, email, account name, SSN, DL number)
   - Commercial information (purchase history, tendencies)
   - Biometric information
   - Internet/Network activity (browsing history, search history)
   - Geolocation data
   - Professional/employment information
   - Inferences drawn from any of the above

2. RIGHT TO DELETE (CCPA § 1798.105)
   Please delete all personal information you have collected about me
   from your records and from the records of any service providers or
   contractors. This includes, but is not limited to:
   - My name and any aliases
   - My current and historical addresses
   - My phone numbers
   - My email addresses
   - My date of birth
   - Photos of me
   - Information about my family members and associates
   - Any records of my employment, education, or financial history

3. RIGHT TO OPT OUT OF SALE/SHARING (CCPA § 1798.120, § 1798.135)
   Please do not sell or share my personal information with any third
   party. This includes any "cross-context behavioral advertising" as
   defined by the CPRA.

VERIFICATION OF IDENTITY:

To verify my identity, I am providing the following information. Please
match this against your records:

- Full legal name: [Your Name]
- Current address: [Street, City, State ZIP]
- Date of birth: [DOB, e.g. MM/DD/YYYY]
- Last 4 of SSN: XXX-XX-XXXX

If you require additional verification, please specify what is needed.
You may contact me at [phone/email] if you have questions.

I understand that you have 45 days to respond to my Right to Know
request, and 45 days to comply with my Right to Delete request. If
additional time is needed, you may extend by an additional 45 days
with notice to me.

If you are unable to comply with this request, please provide a written
explanation citing the specific legal basis for denial.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
[Date]
```

## Generic Opt-Out Letter (For Non-California Residents, or for Brokers That Don't Honor CCPA)

```
[Date]

[Company Name]
[Privacy Office]
[Company Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

Re: Request to Remove Personal Information from Your Database

To Whom It May Concern:

I am writing to request the removal of my personal information from
your database and from any databases you operate or maintain.

I do not wish to have my personal information displayed on your
website or shared with any third party.

Please remove the following information from your records:

- Full legal name: [Your Name]
- Any aliases: [List]
- Current and historical addresses: [List, last 10 years]
- Phone numbers: [List]
- Email addresses: [List]
- Date of birth: [DOB]
- Photos: [Any photos you have of me]
- Information about my family members and associates

I understand that you do not require my information for any legitimate
business purpose, as I am not a customer of yours. If you believe
otherwise, please contact me with specifics.

Please confirm in writing within 30 days that my information has been
removed from your database and from any third-party databases you
have shared it with.

If you do not honor this request, I may escalate to the relevant
state Attorney General, the Federal Trade Commission, or pursue
other available legal remedies.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
[Date]
```

## State-Specific Variations

If you are a resident of any of the following states, you have additional opt-out rights. Substitute the state name in the letter:

| State | Law | Right to Delete | Right to Opt Out of Sale | Notes |
|-------|-----|-----------------|--------------------------|-------|
| California | CCPA / CPRA | Yes | Yes | Strongest. 45-day response. |
| Colorado | CPA | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Connecticut | CTDPA | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Virginia | VCDPA | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Utah | UCPA | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Texas | TDPSA (effective 7/1/2024) | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Washington | My Health My Data Act | Limited | Limited | Specific to health data. |
| Oregon | OCPA (effective 7/1/2024) | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Delaware | DPDPA (effective 1/1/2025) | Yes | Yes | 60-day response. |
| Iowa | ICDPA (effective 1/1/2025) | Yes | Yes | 90-day response. |
| New Hampshire | NHPA (effective 1/1/2025) | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| New Jersey | NJDPA (effective 1/15/2025) | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Tennessee | TIPA (effective 7/1/2025) | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |
| Florida | FDBR (effective 7/1/2024) | Limited | Limited | Specific to breaches. |
| Montana | MCDPA (effective 10/1/2024) | Yes | Yes | 45-day response. |

**Source:** International Association of Privacy Professionals (IAPP) state privacy law tracker.

## Brokers With Self-Service Opt-Outs

Most data brokers have self-service opt-out portals. Use the letter ONLY for brokers that:
- Don't have a self-service opt-out
- Refuse to honor a self-service opt-out
- Require additional documentation

The Identity-Eraser workstream plan (`PROJECTS/Identity-Eraser/PLAN.md`) lists ~120-150 brokers across 10 categories. Most have self-service opt-outs. A small number require the letter above.
