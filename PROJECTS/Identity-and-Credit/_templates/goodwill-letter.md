# Goodwill Letter Template

**Use this template when asking a creditor to remove a late payment as a courtesy.** Best used when the user has a generally good payment history with that creditor but had one or a few late payments due to a specific circumstance (medical, job loss, family emergency, etc.).

**Important:** Goodwill letters are requests, not demands. The creditor is not legally required to remove accurate information. The success rate is roughly 10-30% depending on the creditor, the circumstance, and the user's history with them.

---

## Letter Body

```
[Date]

[Creditor Name]
[Creditor Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

Re: Goodwill Request for Removal of Late Payment
    Account ending in [XXXX]
    Account holder: [Your Name]

To Whom It May Concern:

I am writing to request a courtesy adjustment to my account. I have been a
customer of [Creditor Name] since [year] and have generally maintained a
positive payment history with your institution.

I experienced a [describe circumstance: medical emergency / job loss / family
emergency / temporary cash flow issue] during [month/year] that resulted in a
late payment on my account. This was an isolated incident, and I have since
[recovered / taken corrective action / set up automatic payments to prevent
recurrence].

Since that time, I have made every subsequent payment on time. I take my
financial obligations seriously and value my relationship with [Creditor
Name].

I am respectfully requesting that, as a one-time courtesy, you consider
removing the late payment notation from my account. I have enclosed a copy of
my payment history showing consistent on-time payments before and after the
incident.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
[Your Account Number, last 4]
[Your Contact Information]
```

---

## Tips

- **Be honest about the circumstance.** Don't invent a dramatic story. Creditors can spot it.
- **Be specific about the date.** "March 2024" is better than "a few months ago."
- **Show the pattern.** Highlight on-time payments before AND after the late payment.
- **Be brief.** 1 page max. Bureaus and creditors read hundreds of these.
- **Send to the right address.** Many creditors have a separate address for goodwill requests. Call the number on the back of the card to confirm.
- **Don't threaten.** "I will close my account if you don't remove this" is counterproductive.
- **Track in `_trackers/GOODWILL-LETTERS.csv`.** Add a row for each goodwill letter sent.

---

## When to Use

- 1-2 late payments in an otherwise clean history
- Late payment was due to a real, identifiable circumstance
- The user has been a long-term customer of the creditor
- The late payment is the only thing keeping the score down

## When NOT to Use

- Multiple late payments (the pattern is the problem; one removal doesn't fix it)
- Late payments are recent and the user is still struggling
- The user has a history of late payments with this creditor
- The user is using the goodwill letter as a "trick" rather than a genuine request

---

## Follow-Up

If no response in 30 days, send a polite follow-up. If still no response in 60 days, the request is likely declined. The user can try again after 6-12 months of additional on-time payments, when the late payment becomes a smaller portion of the overall history.
