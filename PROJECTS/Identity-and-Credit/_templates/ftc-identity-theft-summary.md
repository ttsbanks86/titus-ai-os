## What NOT to Do

- **Do not pay any fraudulent account.** Even a partial payment can be construed as acknowledging the debt.
- **Do not give personal information to unknown callers.** Verify the caller by hanging up and calling the institution's published number.
- **Do not throw away the police report or FTC report.** Save them permanently. You may need them for years.
- **Do not assume one bureau's removal means all 3.** Each bureau is independent. Verify each.
- **Do not lift the credit freeze without a specific plan.** Keep it in place. Lift only when you need to apply for credit, then re-freeze.

---

## What to Do If Things Get Worse

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| New fraudulent accounts appear | Pull all 3 reports, re-dispute, file supplemental police report |
| Creditor refuses to remove fraudulent account | File CFPB complaint, escalate to state AG, consult consumer protection attorney |
| SSN confirmed compromised (not just used) | File IRS Form 14039, contact SSA, consider SSN lock (E-Verify + Self Lock) |
| Dark web exposure confirmed | Subscribe to identity monitoring (free at `https://www.haveibeenpwned.com/`, paid at LifeLock / IdentityForce) |
| Arrest or legal action in your name | Contact local law enforcement immediately, file supplemental police report, consult defense attorney |
