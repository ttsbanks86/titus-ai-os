## Template Usage Guidelines

### Do's
- ✅ Always customize with specific details
- ✅ Test different versions for performance
- ✅ Update templates quarterly based on results
- ✅ Keep tone consistent with brand voice
- ✅ Track which templates work best

### Don'ts
- ❌ Send templates without personalization
- ❌ Use outdated information
- ❌ Skip the proofreading step
- ❌ Ignore template performance data
- ❌ Mix formal/informal tone

## Creating New Templates

1. Use `Templates/sop-template.md` as base structure
2. Include clear purpose and usage instructions
