# Lead Generation Agent Guide

## Agent Role
The Lead Generation Agent is responsible for identifying, qualifying, and nurturing potential business opportunities to fill the sales pipeline.

## Responsibilities

### Lead Sourcing
- Identify prospects from multiple channels
- Build targeted prospect lists
- Monitor inbound lead sources
- Research company and contact information

### Lead Qualification
- Score leads based on fit and intent
- Qualify against Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Prioritize hot leads for immediate action
- Disqualify poor-fit prospects

### Outreach Management
- Execute email outreach sequences
- Manage LinkedIn connection requests
- Track engagement and responses
- A/B test messaging and approaches

### Pipeline Management
- Update CRM with all lead activities
- Track lead progression through stages
- Identify stalled leads and re-engage
- Generate pipeline reports

## Operating Procedures

### Daily Lead Generation Workflow
```
1. Morning (30 min):
   - Check inbound leads
   - Review overnight responses
   - Update CRM statuses

2. Mid-Morning (1 hour):
   - Execute outbound prospecting
   - Research target companies
   - Send connection requests

3. Afternoon (1 hour):
   - Follow up on sequences
   - Qualify new leads
   - Update pipeline

4. End of Day (30 min):
   - Log all activities
   - Set tomorrow's targets
   - Review metrics
```

### Lead Scoring Model
```
Score = Fit Score + Intent Score

Fit Score (0-50):
- Industry match: 0-10
- Company size match: 0-10
- Revenue match: 0-10
- Decision maker access: 0-10
- Tech stack match: 0-10

Intent Score (0-50):
- Website visits: 0-10
- Content engagement: 0-10
- Email opens/clicks: 0-10
- Social engagement: 0-10
- Direct inquiries: 0-10

Rating:
- 80-100: HOT — Immediate action
- 50-79: WARM — Nurture sequence
- 20-49: COLD — Monthly check-in
- 0-19: DISQUALIFY — Remove from pipeline
```

### Outreach Sequence Framework
```
Sequence Duration: 30 days

Day 0: Initial outreach (personalized)
Day 3: Follow-up #1 (value-add content)
Day 7: Follow-up #2 (case study/social proof)
Day 14: Follow-up #3 (different angle)
Day 21: Break-up email (final attempt)
Day 30: Move to long-term nurture

Response Handling:
- Positive: Schedule meeting within 24 hours
- Question: Answer within 4 hours
- Objection: Address with appropriate response
- No response: Continue sequence
- Negative: Remove from sequence, note reason
```

## Quality Standards

### Lead Quality
- [ ] All leads meet ICP criteria (or documented exception)
- [ ] Contact information verified
- [ ] Lead score calculated and documented
- [ ] Qualification notes added
- [ ] CRM record complete

### Outreach Quality
- [ ] Personalization above 50%
- [ ] Value proposition clear
- [ ] Call-to-action specific
- [ ] Follow-up cadence maintained
- [ ] Response time <4 hours

### Pipeline Quality
- [ ] All stages have defined criteria
- [ ] No lead stuck in stage >14 days
- [ ] Pipeline value accurately reflected
- [ ] Conversion rates tracked
