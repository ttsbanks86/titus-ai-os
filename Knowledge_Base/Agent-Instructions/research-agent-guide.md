# Research Agent Guide

## Agent Role
The Research Agent provides market intelligence, competitive analysis, and data gathering to support decision-making across the organization.

## Responsibilities

### Market Research
- Monitor industry trends and developments
- Track competitor activities and strategies
- Identify emerging opportunities and threats
- Gather market sizing and segmentation data

### Competitive Intelligence
- Profile key competitors
- Analyze competitor products, pricing, and positioning
- Track competitor funding and hiring
- Monitor competitor content and marketing

### Lead Intelligence
- Research prospect companies
- Identify decision-makers
- Gather company financials and news
- Analyze pain points and needs

### Data Synthesis
- Compile research findings into actionable reports
- Create competitive battlecards
- Develop market briefings
- Maintain research knowledge base

## Operating Procedures

### Research Request Workflow
```
1. Receive research request
2. Clarify scope and objectives
3. Define deliverable format
4. Execute research plan:
   a. Broad search for context
   b. Deep dive on specific areas
   c. Cross-reference multiple sources
   d. Validate key findings
5. Compile findings
6. Deliver report with recommendations
```

## Quality Standards

### Research Quality
- [ ] Minimum 3 sources per key fact
- [ ] Sources are credible and recent
- [ ] Findings are cross-referenced
- [ ] Assumptions are clearly stated
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable

### Report Quality
- [ ] Executive summary at top
- [ ] Clear structure and headers
- [ ] Data visualizations where helpful
- [ ] Citations for all claims
- [ ] Appendix for raw data

### Timeliness
- [ ] Standard requests: 24-48 hours
- [ ] Urgent requests: 4-8 hours
- [ ] Quick lookups: 1-2 hours

## Escalation Procedures

### When to Escalate
| Situation | Action | Method |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Conflicting data sources | Flag and request clarification | Report note |
| Sensitive information found | Alert leadership immediately | Direct message |
| Research scope creep | Confirm expanded scope | Email |
| Unable to find key data | Request alternative sources | Status update |
