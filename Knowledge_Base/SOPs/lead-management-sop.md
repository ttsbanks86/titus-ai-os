# Lead Management SOP

## 1. Purpose
Define the lead lifecycle from capture to conversion, ensuring no lead is lost and every opportunity is properly nurtured.

## 2. Scope
All inbound and outbound leads across all channels (email, web forms, LinkedIn, referrals, cold outreach).

## 3. Prerequisites
- Lead tracking system (CRM or spreadsheet)
- LinkedIn automation access
- Email templates from `Templates/email-templates.md`
- Research agent capabilities

## 4. Procedure

### Step 1: Lead Capture
```
1. New lead identified from any channel
2. Create lead record with:
   - Name
   - Company
   - Email
   - Phone (if available)
   - Source (LinkedIn, email, referral, website)
   - Date captured
   - Initial notes
3. Apply tag: [NEW]
```

### Step 2: Qualification (Within 24 Hours)
```
1. Research lead using research-agent:
   - Company size and industry
   - Recent news or funding
   - Decision-maker identification
   - Pain points analysis
2. Score lead (1-10):
   - 8-10: HOT — immediate action
   - 5-7: WARM — nurture sequence
   - 1-4: COLD — monthly check-in
3. Update lead record with score
```

### Step 3: Outreach
```
1. Select appropriate template from email-templates.md
2. Personalize with research findings
3. Send initial outreach
4. Schedule follow-up sequence:
   - Day 0: Initial outreach
   - Day 3: Follow-up #1
   - Day 7: Follow-up #2
   - Day 14: Final follow-up
   - Day 30: Long-term nurture
```

### Step 4: Pipeline Management
```
Stages:
1. NEW — Just captured
2. CONTACTED — Initial outreach sent
3. ENGAGED — Lead responded
4. QUALIFIED — Meets criteria
5. PROPOSAL — Proposal sent
6. NEGOTIATION — In discussion
7. CLOSED-WON — Deal secured
8. CLOSED-LOST — Did not convert

Update stage in CRM for every interaction.
```

### Step 5: Handoff to Sales
```
1. When lead reaches QUALIFIED stage
2. Prepare handoff document:
   - Lead summary
   - Interaction history
   - Pain points identified
   - Proposed solution
   - Recommended next steps
3. Schedule intro call/meeting
```

## 5. Quality Checks
- [ ] All leads captured within 4 hours of contact
- [ ] Qualification completed within 24 hours
- [ ] Follow-up cadence maintained (no gaps)
- [ ] CRM updated after every interaction
- [ ] Pipeline stages accurate and current

## 6. Escalation Path
| Issue | Escalate To | Method |
|-------|------------|--------|
| Lead non-responsive after 30 days | Sales Manager | Pipeline review |
| High-value lead (>$50k) | Executive Team | Direct briefing |
| Legal/compliance concern | Legal | Email with documentation |
| Technical objection | Solutions Engineer | Handoff meeting |
