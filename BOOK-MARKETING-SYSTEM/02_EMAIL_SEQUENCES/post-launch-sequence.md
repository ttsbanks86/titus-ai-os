## Sequence Settings

### Timing
- **Send Time:** 10:00 AM (recipient's timezone)
- **Send Days:** Tuesday
- **Frequency:** 1 email per week

### Segmentation
- **Primary:** Purchasers
- **Secondary:** Non-purchasers (nurture toward purchase)
- **Tertiary:** Reviewers (VIP treatment)

### Tracking
- **Open Rate Goal:** 30%+
- **Click Rate Goal:** 8%+
- **Reply Rate Goal:** 3%+

### Automation Rules
1. If replied → Personal response within 24 hours
2. If requested speaking → Add to speaking queue
3. If shared on social → Send thank you + bonus content
4. If purchased multiple copies → Add to VIP list
