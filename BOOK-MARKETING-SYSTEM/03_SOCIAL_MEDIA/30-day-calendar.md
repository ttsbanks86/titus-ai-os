## Platform-Specific Notes

### Facebook
- **Format:** Long-form posts (200-300 words)
- **Images:** 1200x630px
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant hashtags
- **Engagement:** Ask questions, respond to comments

### Instagram
- **Format:** Visual-first (images + short captions)
- **Images:** 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait)
- **Stories:** Use stickers, polls, questions
- **Hashtags:** 10-15 relevant hashtags

### Twitter
- **Format:** Short, punchy (280 characters)
- **Images:** 1200x675px
- **Threads:** Use for longer content
- **Hashtags:** 1-3 relevant hashtags

### TikTok
- **Format:** 15-60 second videos
- **Style:** Authentic, raw, personal
- **Music:** Use trending sounds
- **Captions:** Add text overlays

### LinkedIn
- **Format:** Professional, thought-leadership
- **Images:** 1200x627px
- **Content:** Industry insights, publishing journey
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 professional hashtags
