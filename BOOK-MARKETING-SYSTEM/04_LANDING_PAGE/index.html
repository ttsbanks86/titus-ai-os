Struck Down but Not Destroyed

You may be struck down, but through Christ, you are not destroyed.

In a world that promises everything but delivers nothing, Bonolo Morake found herself lost in the chaos of nightclubbing, drinking, and empty relationships. She was living the life she thought she wanted until she discovered it was leading her straight to destruction.

Struck Down but Not Destroyed is a raw, honest journey of leaving everything behind to follow Jesus including a five-year relationship and the life she once knew.

Through heartbreak, rejection, illness, and the hospitalization of her newborn son, she clung to the promises of God. With each trial, her faith grew stronger. With each tear, her character was refined.

"For we are hard-pressed on every side, yet not crushed; perplexed, but not in despair; persecuted, but not forsaken; struck down, but not destroyed."

2 Corinthians 4:8-9
