# Brand Voice

## Overview
Voice, tone, and messaging rules for Titus Banks brands. This document defines how communications should sound across platforms.

## Core Voice Principles
- Authentic, not corporate
- Knowledgeable but accessible
- Personal and story-driven
- Values-aligned: faith, family, integrity
- Professional without being stiff

## Writing Guidelines
- No m dashes in 01-Dashboard content
- Short paragraphs, one idea per paragraph
- Storytelling style where appropriate
- Clear, direct, action-oriented

## Platform Variations
- LinkedIn: Professional, thought-leadership oriented
- X/Twitter: Concise, engaging, conversational
- YouTube: Educational, structured, enthusiastic
- Blog: Story-driven, detailed, personal
