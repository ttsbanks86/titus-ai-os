# Content Guidelines

## Overview
Platform-specific content rules and best practices for each content channel.

## Platform Rules
- LinkedIn: No hashtag stuffing, value-first posts, professional tone
- X/Twitter: 280-char thinking, thread-capable topics, engagement hooks
- YouTube: SEO-optimized titles and descriptions, clear CTAs
- Newsletter: Personal voice, curated value, consistent schedule
- Blog: In-depth topics, 1000-2500 words, storytelling approach

## Content Safety
- No auto-posting without approval
- Review content before publishing
- Keep personal boundaries clear
