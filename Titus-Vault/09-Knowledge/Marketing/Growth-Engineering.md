# Growth Engineering

## Overview
Growth tactics, experiments, and metrics tracking. Growth engineering focuses on data-driven approaches to audience building and content optimization.

## Current State
Growth is in early stages — focusing on establishing consistent content production and measuring basic engagement metrics.

## Growth Levers
- Content quality and consistency
- Cross-platform repurposing
- Engagement and community building
- SEO optimization for YouTube and blog
- Collaboration and networking

## Metrics Tracked
- Post engagement (likes, comments, shares)
- Follower growth rate
- Website/blog traffic
- Newsletter subscribers
- Content repurposing efficiency
