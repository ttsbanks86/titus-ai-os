# Marketing Strategy

## Overview
Overall marketing approach and channel strategy for Titus Banks products and brands.

## Current State
Marketing is content-driven with focus on LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and YouTube. Budget is minimal — growth through quality content and organic reach.

## Channel Strategy
- LinkedIn: Professional networking, B2B connections, career content
- X/Twitter: AI systems discussion, tech community, quick thoughts
- YouTube: Educational content, tutorials, product demos
- Newsletter: Direct audience connection, deeper content

## Key Principles
- Value-first: every piece of content provides value
- Consistency: regular posting schedule
- Repurposing: one idea, multiple formats
- Engagement: conversation over broadcasting
