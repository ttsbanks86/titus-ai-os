# My Goals

## Who I Am at My Core
I am a father, a minister, a first-generation college graduate, and a fast learner. I am dependable, easygoing, and driven by purpose. My life is not just about me. It is about breaking generational cycles and building a legacy for my children and their children.

## Financial Goals
- **Immediate:** Clear approximately $80,000 in debt
- **Short-term:** Earn at least $100,000+ in savings as a foundation
- **Long-term:** Generate $50,000/month through multiple streams of income
- **Freedom definition:** Multiple income streams that allow me to travel the world without worrying about finances
- **Current reality:** Earning ~$58,000/year in Seattle, WA. Near the poverty line for this city. This is temporary.

## Career Goals
- **Next 12 months:** Land a stable, well-paying job in tech (cybersecurity or product management) OR be actively working on a successful business
- **Why cybersecurity:** It is in demand, stable for at least the next 20 years, and aligns with my fast-learning nature
- **Path:** Using Riipen internship, PM portfolio, and WGU education to position myself for a career transition
- **Ultimate vision:** Work on meaningful projects, lead teams, and build something that creates generational wealth

## Family Goals
- Give my 5 children (4 boys, 1 girl) a childhood free from financial compromise
- Provide a safer, more comfortable home environment
- Pass on generational wealth to my children and grandchildren
- Be remembered as the man who got his family out of poverty and broke generational curses
- Support my oldest son (17, graduating soon with a Bachelor's in IT Management) as he launches his career

## Ministry Goals
- Return to ministry within the next 12 months. On a fresh, clean slate.
- Build an independent ministry not under any other organization
- Use the Masters in Divinity to preach with knowledge and understanding
- Ministry is my calling. Not a job, not a hobby. It is who I am.
- Combine faith, knowledge, and leadership to impact people's lives

## Legacy Goal
I want to be known as someone who worked hard, got his family out of poverty, broke generational curses, and took his family to another level. I am the first graduate in my family. That record has already been set. Everything from here is building on that foundation.
