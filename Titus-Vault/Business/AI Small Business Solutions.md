# AI Small Business Solutions

## Mission

Research, validate, build, and sell practical AI systems that solve measurable problems for small businesses.

## Validation sequence

1. Identify a specific customer segment.
2. Document a costly, frequent, measurable problem.
3. Validate willingness to pay.
4. Build the smallest useful system.
5. Measure the result and iterate.

Old product ideas are supporting research and do not become active unless promoted through [[Decision Log]].
