# NOLA Voice

## Overview
A voice assistant for desktop control. NOLA Voice enables hands-free computer operation through natural language voice commands — opening applications, controlling windows, executing workflows.

## Current State
- Phase: Concept / Planning
- Last updated: 2026-06-21
- Relationship: Part of the voice-controlled ecosystem with EchoKeys and Whisper Pro

## Goals
- Enable full desktop control through voice
- Integrate with Personal AI Operator for complex workflows
- Support custom command creation
