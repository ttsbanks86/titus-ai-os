## Claim policy

Each claim must be classified as **Verified**, **Supported**, **Needs Evidence**, **Remove**, or **Historical Only** in [[Titus/Career/Resume Claim Evidence Matrix|Resume Claim Evidence Matrix]]. Unsupported metrics, dollar savings, team sizes, promotion claims, system ownership, and technical skills are not approved.

Academic work, simulations, experiential learning, and personal projects must never be presented as paid employment.
