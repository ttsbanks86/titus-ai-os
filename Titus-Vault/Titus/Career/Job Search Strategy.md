# Job Search Strategy

## Objective

Obtain stable employment. Apply selectively to roles that meet the target profile, tailor every resume, and track every application and follow-up.

## Search order

1. Remote roles.
2. Dallas roles.
3. Seattle roles when appropriate.

## Operating rules

- Primary role: Business Analyst.
- Secondary roles: [[Target Roles]].
- Salary target: at least $70,000.
- Score jobs before applying; the current documented threshold is 75/100.
- Never auto-apply, auto-send, or invent qualifications.
- Use [[Resume Source of Truth]] and record activity in [[Job Application Tracker]].
- Review recruiter and networking activity weekly.

## Weekly cadence

- Identify and score qualified openings.
- Tailor resumes and cover letters.
- Apply and record outcomes.
- Follow up on appropriate applications.
- Practice interviews using [[Interview Preparation]] and [[STAR Story Bank]].
