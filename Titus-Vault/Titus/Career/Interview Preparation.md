# Interview Preparation

## Before every interview

- Confirm role, company, interviewers, format, and time zone.
- Map the job requirements to verified experience.
- Select three to five stories from [[STAR Story Bank]].
- Prepare questions about success measures, team structure, priorities, and hiring timeline.
- Rehearse a concise career-transition narrative.

## After every interview

- Record questions asked, responses, weak areas, follow-up, and decision in [[Job Search Decision Log]].
