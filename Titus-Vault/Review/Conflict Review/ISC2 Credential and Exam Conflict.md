# ISC2 Credential and Exam Conflict

## Resolution

The Pearson VUE evidence verifies a rescheduled and scheduled ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity exam appointment for July 28, 2026 at 9:30 AM Pacific.

It does **not** verify that the exam was taken, passed, or that the credential was awarded. The earlier ambiguity is resolved by keeping registration, scheduling, completion, passing, and award as separate stages.
