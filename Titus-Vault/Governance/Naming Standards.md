# Naming Standards

- Use clear, human-readable titles.
- Name authoritative notes for the topic, not the date of discovery.
- Use `YYYY-MM-DD` for daily notes and date-led records.
- Avoid `Untitled`, ambiguous acronyms, and version words such as `final-final`.
- Preserve aliases when a rename is approved.
- Do not rename solely for visual consistency; retrieval and link stability come first.
