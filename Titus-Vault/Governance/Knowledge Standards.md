# Knowledge Standards

TKOS optimizes for reliable retrieval, clear thinking, and preserved history. Relationships are more important than visual folder organization.

## Required principles

1. Search before creating.
2. Maintain one authoritative note per durable topic.
3. Link supporting notes to an authoritative hub.
4. Preserve historical information and document supersession.
5. Put uncertain ownership or classification in `Review`.
6. Never permanently delete knowledge during stewardship work.
7. Process migrations in validated batches of no more than 100 notes.

## Note lifecycle

Capture → classify → connect → review → maintain → archive.

Daily notes are historical capture records. Durable knowledge must be promoted into permanent notes without erasing the original daily entry.
