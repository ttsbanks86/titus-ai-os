**Owner:** CDO (exec-cdo)

---

## Why This Quick Win

The audit (Section 7.1 of `BRAND-SYSTEM/Asset-Audit-Report.md`) flagged the BA Carousel as production-ready overall (70/75), but slide 1 was the weakest link:

- **Current state:** Generic stock-photo-feel background with text "Stop Guessing. Start Knowing."
- **Problem:** Doesn't pass the "could I send this to a CIO?" test. The rest of the carousel is strong (8.5/10), but slide 1 sets the click-through tone.
- **Goal:** Replace the background visual with something that signals "real Business Analyst at work" — a desk, a process diagram, a wireframe, a data table — not a stock pose.

Estimated swap time: 5-10 minutes. Estimated impact: higher LinkedIn CTR, better brand consistency across the 10-slide set.

---

## The CEO's 5-Minute VistaCreate Workflow

### Step 1: Open VistaCreate

1. Go to https://create.vista.com
2. Sign in.
3. Open the existing BA Carousel design (the 10-slide set, currently scoring 70/75 in the audit).
4. The audit references it at `NETLIFY-DROP/ba/`. If the source design is in VistaCreate, search "BA Carousel" in the user's VistaCreate library.

### Step 2: Navigate to Slide 1

1. In the VistaCreate editor, click slide 1 of 10 in the left panel.
2. Identify the current background: a photographic image that looks like a stock businessperson or generic office scene.
3. Click the background to select it.

### Step 3: Replace the Background

Choose ONE of these replacement options (CDO recommendation: **Option A**):

**Option A — Wireframe/Sketch Background (CDO recommended)**
- Click "Background" > "Photos" in VistaCreate.
- Search: "wireframe sketch" or "process diagram hand drawn" or "whiteboard flowchart."
- Pick an image that shows: clean lines, neutral cream/white background, hand-drawn or geometric shapes, no people.
- Why: signals "this is how a BA thinks" without being a stock pose.

**Option B — Real BA Work Image**
- Search: "spreadsheet business" or "data table analytics" or "kanban board."
- Pick an image of a screen with real-looking data (numbers, charts, columns).
- Why: shows the output of BA work.

**Option C — Process Diagram**
- Search: "workflow diagram" or "bpmn" or "process flow."
- Pick an image that shows a clean flowchart with steps.
- Why: shows the methodology.

**Option D — Keep the current background but add a 60% navy scrim**
- Click the background, then in the right panel find "Adjust" or "Filters."
- Add a navy color overlay at 60% opacity.
- Why: instantly makes the existing image more on-brand and reduces the stock-photo feel.

### Step 4: Verify the Text Still Reads

- Confirm the headline "Stop Guessing. Start Knowing." is still legible.
- If the new background is busy, add a cream or navy rectangle behind the text at 80-90% opacity.
- Confirm the gold accent on key words still pops.

### Step 5: Export and Replace

1. Click "Download" in VistaCreate.
2. Choose PNG.
3. Save as: `BA-Carousel-Slide-01-Rev1.png`
4. Move the file to: `BRAND/Asset-Library/BA/` (create the folder if it doesn't exist).
5. (Optional but recommended) Re-export the entire 10-slide carousel as a PDF and save it to the same folder as `BA-Carousel-10slides-Rev1.pdf`.

### Step 6: Report Back

Reply to the CDO with:
1. Which option (A, B, C, or D) you chose.
2. The saved file path.
3. A 1-sentence "did it work" note.

The CDO will:
- Update `BRAND/Asset-Library-Index.md` (asset A2) with the new file.
- Bump the score (estimated 72 → 75/75 if option A or D, 73/75 if B, 74/75 if C).
- Move BA Carousel from "needs attention" to "fully production-ready" status.

## If the CEO Decides Not to Do the Swap

That is fine. The asset is still production-ready at 70/75. The swap is a quality upgrade, not a blocker. Mark this QW as "deferred" in the Phase 0 report and revisit during Phase 3 (Pitch Deck Rebuild) when BA visuals get a full pass.

---

## Risks

- **VistaCreate design lost or deleted.** The CDO will not be able to recover a deleted VistaCreate design. The CEO should ensure the original is saved before starting, or work on a copy.
- **Text legibility regression.** Some replacement backgrounds can make text harder to read. The scrim option (D) is the safest.
- **Brand drift.** Search results in VistaCreate can pull from outside the brand palette. Stick to cream/white/navy backgrounds. Avoid bright reds, greens, blues, gradients.
