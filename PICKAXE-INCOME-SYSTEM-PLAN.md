## Best Low-Maintenance Income Pilots

### 1. Lead Leak Audit Assistant
Audience: local service businesses, contractors, clinics, med spas, cleaners, landscapers, legal offices.

Offer:
- Free/public 5-use audit assistant that asks for website, phone process, missed-call handling, lead source, and follow-up process.
- Produces a Lead Leak Score, a missed revenue estimate, and a prioritized fix plan.
- Upgrade path sends users to book an audit call or buy a paid audit/report.

Why this fits:
- Matches the existing Lead Leak Recovery System.
- Can be used as a lead magnet and sales qualifier.
- Does not require heavy daily maintenance once the prompt and portal are set.

Suggested setup:
- Public Guest access: 3–5 uses/month.
- Limit reached behavior: link to booking page or paid audit page.
- Paid tier: one-time audit or monthly member access if Stripe is connected.
- Knowledge base: local service lead follow-up checklist, missed-call SMS templates, compliance basics, CRM setup checklist.

### 2. Student Paper / Study Coach Portal
Audience: students who need structure, outlining, APA help, study plans, and rubric interpretation.

Offer:
- A portal with multiple study/assignment tools:
  - Essay Outline Builder.
  - APA Citation Helper.
  - Rubric Decoder.
  - NCLEX-PN Practice Generator.
  - 30-Day Study Plan Generator.

Why this fits:
- Current account already has NCLEX and academic essay agents.
- Existing usage history proves the tools are usable.
- Can sell as low-ticket access or use as an affiliate funnel for education tools.

Suggested setup:
- Keep it ethical: help with outlines, structure, citations, review, and study plans — not dishonest ghostwriting.
- Public free trial with 5 uses/month.
- Member tier for extra uses.
- Add a clear disclaimer and academic integrity guidance.

### 3. Content Metadata / Affiliate Content Assistant
Audience: creators, churches, coaches, small business owners, affiliate marketers.

Offer:
- A tool that turns transcripts or rough notes into:
  - Titles.
  - Descriptions.
  - Hashtags.
  - Short captions.
  - YouTube/TikTok/Facebook variants.
  - Affiliate product angle ideas.

Why this fits:
- Uses the NOLO/Brand2Social workflow knowledge without depending on that workflow.
- Can become a template/tool product for creators.
- Low maintenance because users bring their own transcript/content.

Suggested setup:
- Embed on a simple landing page.
- Public free trial with 3–5 uses.
- Upgrade path to buy more uses or request a done-for-you content batch.

## Recommended First Build
