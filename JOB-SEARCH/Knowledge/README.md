## Common Questions To Prepare (Standard Application Answers)

Work authorization:
- "Are you authorized to work in the United States?" -> Yes
- "Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?" -> No (or Yes if applicable)

Salary:
- "What is your salary expectation?" -> $75,000-$95,000 depending on full comp package
- "What is your current salary?" -> Prefer to discuss total comp, not just base

Start date:
- "When can you start?" -> 2 weeks from offer acceptance

Relocation:
- "Are you willing to relocate?" -> Yes, to Dallas TX
- "Do you require relocation assistance?" -> Open to discussing

Remote work:
- "Do you prefer remote work?" -> Open to remote, hybrid, or on-site in target cities

Background:
- "Have you been convicted of a felony?" -> No (or answer honestly)

EEO:
- Voluntary self-identification. Skip if prefer not to say.

## Application Question Patterns (Save Answers In Per-Job Folder)

When the same question appears at multiple companies, save the answer to `Knowledge/COMMON_ANSWERS.md` so you never rewrite it.

Examples:
- "Why do you want to work here?" (always customize)
- "Tell me about yourself" (60-second pitch)
- "What's your greatest weakness?" (real, with mitigation)
- "Why are you leaving your current role?" (positive framing)
- "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" (growth aligned with role)
