## Resume Style & Editing Workflow

**Tone:** Clear, direct, warm, practical, grounded, human. No generic AI phrasing. No corporate filler. No hype. No motivational fluff. No emojis. No em dashes.

**Banned words (Titus Banks brand):** "Elevate", "Seamless", "Unleash", "Next-Gen". Avoid in all resume and cover letter content.

**Length:** One page for private/public sector. Two to four pages for federal (USAJobs) format.

**Format conventions:**
- Master resume: keep both versions, do not mix
  - `Titus_Banks_MASTER_PM_Private.docx` - private/public/non-profit
  - `Titus_Banks_MASTER_PM_Federal.docx` - federal government only
- Tailored resume: derived from master, lives in `Current Job Application/[Job]/`
- Always keep `.md` source alongside `.docx` (Markdown for editing, DOCX for ATS)

**Editing workflow (two-AI):**
- **OpenCode (this CLI):** file management, tailoring structure, format conversion, tracker updates
- **Claude (browser at claude.ai):** narrative review, bullet tightening, content polish, keyword surfacing
- **Human (Titus):** final review, final approval, final submit

Full rules in `Resume/RESUME_MANAGEMENT.md`.

**Federal vs private rule:**
- If posting says "federal resume format", "USAJobs format", or is on USAJobs.gov -> Federal master
- Anything else -> Private master
- When in doubt, ask before submitting
