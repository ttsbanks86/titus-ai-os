# Job Description — Entry Level Business Data Analyst

**Company:** Data Cloud Merge
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Salary:** $78,000 - $85,200/year
**Type:** Full-time

## About the Role
Looking for a curious and analytical professional who enjoys solving business problems using data. Partner with cross-functional teams to transform raw data into actionable insights, build dashboards, and support strategic business decisions through reporting and analysis.

Excellent opportunity for recent graduates and early-career professionals looking to build a career in Business Analytics and Data Intelligence.

## What You'll Do
- Analyze business and operational datasets to identify trends, risks, and growth opportunities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders and translate them into analytical solutions
- Design interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI or Tableau
- Write SQL queries to extract, validate, and manipulate data from relational databases
- Perform data profiling, cleansing, and quality validation to ensure reporting accuracy
- Create KPI metrics and business performance reports for leadership teams
- Support process improvement initiatives through data-driven recommendations
- Collaborate with business users, analysts, and technical teams in an Agile environment
- Present analytical findings through clear visualizations and executive-friendly presentations
- Utilize AI-powered productivity tools responsibly to enhance reporting and analysis workflows

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, Information Systems, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, or a related field
- 0-2 years of experience
- Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
